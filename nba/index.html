In-market vs. out-of-market

In your team's market: Most regular-season games air only inside the team's home territory, on its regional sports network, a local TV station, or the team's own streaming app. Games picked for national TV air on ESPN/ABC, NBC/Peacock or Prime Video.

Out of market: Outside the team's territory, NBA League Pass streams games that aren't on national TV. Local games are blacked out on League Pass in the teams' home markets.
