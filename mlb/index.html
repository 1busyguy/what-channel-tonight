In-market vs. out-of-market

In your team's market: Most games air inside the team's home territory on its regional network, a local station, or MLB's in-market streaming. National games air on the networks listed (FOX/FS1, ESPN, TBS and others), and postseason games are always national.

Out of market: Outside the team's market, MLB.TV streams regular-season games that aren't on national TV, with local blackouts.
