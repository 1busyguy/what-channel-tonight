## Why bowl games are confusing to find on TV

- **Several networks, not one:** most bowls are on ESPN or ABC, but the Sun Bowl is on CBS, three bowls are on The CW, and four playoff games (two first-round games, the Fiesta Bowl and the Orange Bowl) are on TNT and truTV.
- **TNT isn't on an antenna:** TNT and truTV are cable channels. If you watch with an antenna, you'll miss those four CFP games unless you add a service that carries TNT or stream them on HBO Max.
- **Weekday afternoon kickoffs:** many bowls start at 11 a.m., 1:30 p.m. or 2 p.m. ET on weekdays. That's 8 a.m. to 11 a.m. on the West Coast.
- **Teams are announced late:** you can plan by channel now, but you won't know who plays where until Sunday, Dec. 6.

## How to watch the bowl games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** ABC, CBS and The CW are broadcast networks in most markets. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) picks up the ABC bowls, the national championship, the Sun Bowl on CBS and The CW's three bowls if you're in range.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the ESPN app on the right plan streams ESPN's bowl games. The CW says its bowl games also stream in the ESPN app for ESPN Unlimited subscribers.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/):** carry ABC, CBS, ESPN, TNT and truTV in most markets, which covers almost every bowl and playoff game. Check The CW for your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN and TNT. Pair it with an antenna for ABC, CBS and The CW.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ABC, CBS and ESPN in most markets. Check whether your plan has TNT and truTV before you count on it for the playoff.
- **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/):** streams TNT Sports' College Football Playoff games, including the Fiesta Bowl and Orange Bowl.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/):** streams the Sun Bowl on CBS in most markets.

Channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For more help, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
