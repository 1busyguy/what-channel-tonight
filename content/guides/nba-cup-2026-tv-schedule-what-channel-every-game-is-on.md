**Short answer:** the NBA Cup doesn't have a single channel. On Friday Cup Nights, two games a night are national on **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/)** and the rest are on each team's **local TV channel or team stream** (out-of-market fans can use [NBA League Pass](/go/nba-league-pass/)). The extra Cup Nights are the exceptions: **Tuesday, Nov 24** has Celtics at Wizards on **NBC and [Peacock](/go/peacock/)** and Mavericks at Nuggets on **Peacock**, and **Wednesday, Nov 25** has three games on **ESPN**. The knockout rounds start Dec. 4, and the Dec. 11 final is at **Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis**. Our [NBA tonight page](/nba/) shows the channel for every game each night.

## 2026 NBA Cup groups

All 30 teams were drawn into six five-team groups within their conference. Each team plays everyone in its group once, two at home and two on the road.

| Group | Teams |
|---|---|
| East Group A | Pistons, Raptors, Magic, Bucks, Nets |
| East Group B | Knicks, Cavaliers, 76ers, Heat, Pacers |
| East Group C | Celtics, Hawks, Hornets, Bulls, Wizards |
| West Group A | Nuggets, Rockets, Suns, Mavericks, Jazz |
| West Group B | Thunder, Timberwolves, Clippers, Pelicans, Grizzlies |
| West Group C | Spurs, Lakers, Trail Blazers, Warriors, Kings |

The six group winners and one wild card per conference (the best second-place team) move on to an eight-team knockout bracket.

## Why your Cup game might not be on national TV

Most NBA Cup games are regular local broadcasts with a Cup logo on them. If you live in one of the teams' home markets, the game is on that team's local channel. Some teams now air games on free local stations (like KJZZ for the Jazz, KSBI for the Thunder, KTLA for the Clippers or KUNP for the Trail Blazers), and several sell their own direct streams (Jazz+, Thunder App, ClipperVision, BlazerVision, MavsTV). Others stream locally on [DAZN](/go/dazn/) or air on a regional sports network you can get through cable, satellite, [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) or [Fubo](/go/fubo/) depending on your ZIP code.

If you live outside both teams' markets, the local-only games are on [NBA League Pass](/go/nba-league-pass/). League Pass blacks out games in the teams' own markets, so it doesn't replace your local channel.

## How to watch the NBA Cup without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** two national games on most Friday Cup Nights, the quarterfinals and the championship.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** Celtics at Wizards and Mavericks at Nuggets on Tuesday, Nov 24.
- **[An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/):** NBC's Celtics at Wizards game, plus games on free local stations in teams that use them.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/), [Sling TV](/go/sling/), [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/) or [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/):** the three ESPN games on Wednesday, Nov 25.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) and [Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carry many regional sports networks for in-market local games (check your ZIP code).
- **[NBA League Pass](/go/nba-league-pass/):** every local-only game when you're out of market.
