## Jimmy V Classic 2026: times and channel

The 32nd Jimmy V Classic is a Madison Square Garden doubleheader on **Tuesday, Dec 8**, and both games are on **ESPN**, per ESPN Events:

- 6:30 p.m. ET: No. 16 Alabama vs No. 8 Houston, **ESPN**
- 9:00 p.m. ET: Georgia vs No. 5 Duke, **ESPN**

The event raises money for the V Foundation for Cancer Research as part of ESPN's V Week. Both games also stream in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/) with a qualifying subscription or TV provider login.

- **Noon to 5 p.m.:** Florida vs Georgia Tech (ESPNU), North Carolina at Georgetown (Peacock), Penn State at Illinois (FS1), St. John's vs Alabama (ESPN, 3:30) and **Louisville at Kentucky (ABC, 4:00)**.

## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Peacock-only:** Illinois at Minnesota, Purdue at Wisconsin, North Carolina at Georgetown and Butler at Providence stream on [Peacock](/go/peacock/), not on NBC or any cable channel.
- **FOX vs FS1:** Tennessee at Purdue, Michigan vs Northwestern, Kansas at UConn and Gonzaga at Creighton are on FOX, the free broadcast network. Utah State at BYU, Iowa State at Iowa, UCF at Indiana, Penn State at Illinois, Seton Hall at Rutgers and Mississippi State at Marquette are on FS1, a separate cable channel.
- **ABC:** Louisville at Kentucky is on ABC, not ESPN, so check your local ABC station or an antenna.
- **The CW:** Gonzaga's Dec 9 home game and Virginia's Dec 13 home game are on The CW, a broadcast network you can usually get free with an antenna.
- **ESPN+ and SEC Network+:** St. John's, Texas Tech, Texas and Arkansas home games against non-conference opponents stream only in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/), not on a TV channel.
- **Conference networks:** Big Ten Network, SEC Network and ACC Network carry Michigan State, Michigan, UCLA, Nebraska, Arkansas, Vanderbilt and Virginia games. Most live TV streaming services carry them, but not every plan does.
- **truTV and TNT Sports:** UConn's Dec 8 game, Maine at DePaul and FIU at Xavier are on truTV, which also streams on [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/).

## How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ACC Network and SEC Network, which carry the Jimmy V Classic and the Houston Hoopfest.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** Illinois at Minnesota, Purdue at Wisconsin and the other Peacock-only games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ABC, FOX, FS1, Big Ten Network, ACC Network, SEC Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the channels in the table, including ABC, FOX, ESPN, ESPN2, FS1 and truTV. Check each lineup for The CW, CBS Sports Network and the conference networks in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and truTV, and Sling Blue has FS1. Check its lineup for ESPNU, ACC Network, SEC Network and CBS Sports Network.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX and FS1, **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/)** streams the truTV games, and **[an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/)** picks up ABC, FOX and The CW free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
