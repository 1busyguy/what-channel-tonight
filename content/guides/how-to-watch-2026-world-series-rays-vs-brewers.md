**Short answer: FOX.** Every game of the 2026 World Series, the Tampa Bay Rays vs the Milwaukee Brewers, airs nationally on **FOX** at **8 p.m. ET**. The Spanish-language broadcast is on **FOX Deportes**, and you can stream every game on **FOX One**. The Brewers won Game 1 3-2 and Game 2 5-4 in Milwaukee and lead the series 2-0.

## Why it's not on your Rays or Brewers channel

During the regular season most [Rays](/mlb/tampa-bay-rays/) and [Brewers](/mlb/milwaukee-brewers/) games air on local TV in each team's home territory. The World Series is different: it's a **national exclusive on FOX**, so local team broadcasts don't carry it, and the channel is the same everywhere in the US. You don't need to be in Tampa Bay or Milwaukee.

## How to watch the World Series without cable

You need a way to get FOX. All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** FOX is a broadcast network. If you're in range of your local FOX station, [an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets every game for free.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/):** FOX's own streaming service, which streams every World Series game.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** a sports-focused live TV service that carries local FOX stations in most markets.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/):** includes local FOX stations in most markets, along with many regional sports networks for the regular season.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Blue carries FOX in select markets only. Check your ZIP code before you sign up.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local FOX stations in most markets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.

## Watching on MLB.TV

[MLB.TV](/go/mlb-tv/) on its own won't stream the World Series. According to MLB, the FOX telecasts are available in the MLB.TV app only to subscribers who also sign in with a participating pay TV provider that carries FOX.

## Spanish-language and radio coverage

- **TV:** FOX Deportes has Spanish-language coverage of every game. Univision also aired Game 1.
- **Radio:** ESPN Radio has national English-language coverage. TUDN Radio carries the Spanish-language radio call.
