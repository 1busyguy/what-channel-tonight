## Sunday Night Football in Mexico City

**Vikings vs 49ers** is played at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City, with San Francisco as the home team. Unlike the early-morning games in Europe, this one stays in the normal prime-time slot: 8:20 p.m. ET, 5:20 p.m. PT. It's on **NBC** nationally, so it's on your local NBC station anywhere in the US. You can watch it free with [an antenna](/go/antenna/), stream it on [Peacock](/go/peacock/), or use a live TV service that carries your local NBC station.

## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With four FOX games and three CBS games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, FOX has a single game (Jets at Chargers) while CBS has two, so most of the country gets either Raiders at Broncos or Steelers at Eagles on CBS. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 11 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday afternoon games and the Mexico City Sunday night game for free if you're in range.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Colts at Texans this week.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Vikings vs 49ers this week.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
