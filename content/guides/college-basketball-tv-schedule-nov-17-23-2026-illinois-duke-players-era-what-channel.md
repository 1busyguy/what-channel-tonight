## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Streaming-only games:** Arkansas vs Indiana at Madison Square Garden and Texas A&M at TCU are on Peacock only, not NBC, so you need [Peacock](/go/peacock/). UCLA and USC's Sunday home games are on B1G+, the Big Ten's own subscription service.
- **ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra:** Texas Tech, BYU and Kansas home games on ESPN+, Alabama, Vanderbilt, Texas, Tennessee and Arkansas home games on SEC Network+, and Miami's Tuesday game on ACC Network Extra stream only in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/). They are not on ESPN, ESPN2 or any cable channel.
- **FS1 vs FOX:** UConn at Arizona and Butler vs Nebraska are on FS1, a cable channel. Michigan at Villanova on Saturday is on the FOX broadcast network.
- **TNT and truTV:** Iowa at Xavier is on TNT, and St. John's, Creighton and Butler home games are on truTV. Live games on both channels also stream on [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/).
- **CBS vs CBS Sports Network:** Kentucky vs Indiana is on CBS, the free broadcast network. Gonzaga's game and the Baha Mar and Greenbrier tournament games are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
- **Conference networks:** Big Ten Network, ACC Network and SEC Network games need a cable or live TV plan with that channel.

## How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the only way to watch the ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU and ACC Network, including Illinois at Duke and the Players Era.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** Arkansas vs Indiana and Texas A&M at TCU.
- **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/):** streams TNT's Iowa at Xavier and truTV's games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, FS1, FOX, CBS, Big Ten Network, ACC Network, SEC Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the TV channels in the table, including CBS, FOX, ESPN, TNT, FS1 and the conference networks. Check each lineup for CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and TNT, and Sling Blue has FS1 and local FOX in some markets. Check its lineup for the conference networks.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams CBS's Kentucky vs Indiana, **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX and FS1, and **[an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/)** picks up CBS and FOX free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
