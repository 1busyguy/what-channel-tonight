## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With four CBS games and four FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. That's why a Ravens fan in Denver or a Cowboys fan in Boston won't find their team on local TV. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The night games are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 8 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday games and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Panthers at Packers this week.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday and Monday night in one service.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local CBS, FOX, NBC and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
