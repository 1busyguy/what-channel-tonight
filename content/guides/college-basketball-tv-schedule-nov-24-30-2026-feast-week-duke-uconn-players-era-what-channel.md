## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Prime Video:** UConn vs Duke streams on [Prime Video](/go/prime-video/), not on a TV channel. You need an Amazon Prime or Prime Video membership.
- **Peacock-only:** Vanderbilt at Ohio State is on [Peacock](/go/peacock/), not NBC.
- **ESPN+ and SEC Network+:** Florida's and Vanderbilt's home games are on SEC Network+, and Houston's Sunday game is on ESPN+. Both stream only in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/), not on ESPN or ESPN2.
- **CBS vs CBS Sports Network:** Michigan State vs Arkansas on Thanksgiving is on CBS, the free broadcast network. The Acrisure Series and Houston's Friday game are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
- **FS1 and FS2:** Texas, UCLA, Purdue, Illinois and UConn's Nov 30 game are on FS1, and Oklahoma vs Pittsburgh is on FS2. Both are cable channels, not the FOX broadcast network.
- **truTV:** Villanova's Tuesday home game is on truTV, which also streams on [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/).

## How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** the only way to watch UConn vs Duke.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPNU, which carry the Players Era and Battle 4 Atlantis.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** Vanderbilt at Ohio State.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, CBS, FS1, FS2, Big Ten Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the channels in the table, including CBS, ESPN, ESPN2, FS1 and truTV. Check each lineup for CBS Sports Network and FS2 in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and truTV, and Sling Blue has FS1. Check its lineup for ESPNU and CBS Sports Network.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams CBS's Michigan State vs Arkansas, **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FS1 and FS2, and **[an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/)** picks up CBS free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
