## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **"ESPN or ESPN2":** ESPN hasn't yet said which of its two main channels gets Florida at Texas, Vanderbilt at Tennessee, Miami at Pittsburgh and SMU at Virginia. If you have one, you almost certainly have the other, and both stream in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/) with a TV provider login or the right ESPN plan.
- **Peacock-only:** Georgetown at UConn, Cornell at Washington, the Rutgers and Northwestern home games on Dec 29, Marquette at Creighton, Ohio State at Iowa, USC at Indiana and Villanova at Georgetown stream on [Peacock](/go/peacock/), not on NBC or any cable channel.
- **NBC vs Peacock:** UConn at Creighton on Jan 3 is on NBC itself, the free broadcast network, so an antenna works for that one.
- **CBS vs CBS Sports Network:** West Virginia at Kansas and Michigan State at Nebraska are on CBS, the broadcast network. Iowa State's Dec 29 home game and Colorado at Oklahoma State are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
- **FOX vs FS1:** Texas Tech at Cincinnati and Illinois at Wisconsin are on FOX, the broadcast network. The Big East games and UCLA's games are on FS1, a cable channel.
- **The CW:** Louisville at Virginia Tech, Cornell at Gonzaga and California at Duke are on The CW, a broadcast network you can usually get free with an antenna.
- **ESPN+, SEC Network+ and B1G+:** the Memphis, BYU, Texas Tech and Houston home games marked ESPN+ and the LSU, Tennessee and Texas games marked SEC Network+ stream only in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/). The UCLA, Indiana and Illinois games marked B1G+ stream only on B1G+, the Big Ten's own streaming service, not on Big Ten Network.
- **Conference networks:** Big Ten Network, SEC Network and ACC Network carry many of the Top 25 home games. Most live TV streaming services carry them, but not every plan does.
- **truTV:** Incarnate Word at Arizona, Fairleigh Dickinson at Villanova and Seton Hall at DePaul are on truTV, which also streams on [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/).

## How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ACC Network and SEC Network, which carry Florida at Texas, Vanderbilt at Tennessee, Duke at Georgia Tech and Arizona at Iowa State.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** Georgetown at UConn, USC at Indiana, Marquette at Creighton and the other Peacock-only games.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/):** streams the CBS games, West Virginia at Kansas and Michigan State at Nebraska, on plans that include live CBS (check the plan before you subscribe).
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/):** streams FOX and FS1, so it covers Texas Tech at Cincinnati, Illinois at Wisconsin and the Big East games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries CBS, FOX, NBC, ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, FS1, Big Ten Network, ACC Network, SEC Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the channels in the table, including CBS, FOX, NBC, ESPN, ESPN2, FS1 and truTV. Check each lineup for The CW, CBS Sports Network and the conference networks in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and truTV, and Sling Blue has FS1. Sling doesn't carry CBS and carries FOX and NBC only in some markets, so pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
- **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/)** streams the truTV games, and **[an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/)** picks up CBS, FOX, NBC and The CW free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
