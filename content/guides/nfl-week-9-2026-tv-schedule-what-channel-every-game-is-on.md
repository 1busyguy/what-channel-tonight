## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With three CBS games and four FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. That's why a Jets fan in Seattle or a Cowboys fan in Boston won't find their team on local TV. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, the early **NFL Network** game, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN/ABC)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 9 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday games, Sunday Night Football and the ABC simulcast of Monday Night Football for free if you're in range.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Jaguars at Ravens this week.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC stations in most markets plus ESPN and NFL Network, so it covers the early NFL Network game, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations, ESPN and NFL Network in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
