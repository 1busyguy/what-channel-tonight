**Short answer:** every NBA Cup knockout game is on **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/)**. The quarterfinals are **76ers at Hawks** (7:00 p.m. ET) and **Spurs at Mavericks** (9:30 p.m. ET) on **Friday, Dec. 4**, then **Celtics at Pistons** (7:30 p.m. ET) and **Grizzlies at Trail Blazers** (10:00 p.m. ET) on **Saturday, Dec. 5**. The semifinals follow on Dec. 8 (and possibly Dec. 9), and the championship is **Friday, Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis**. Our [NBA tonight page](/nba/) shows the channel for every game each night.

## Semifinals and championship

| Round | Date | Matchup | Where | TV / stream |

The NBA's release gives the semifinal dates as "Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9." ESPN's schedule currently has both games on Tuesday, Dec. 8, with tip times still to be set. The NBA announced in June that the championship streams exclusively on Prime Video, and Sportsnet's schedule lists the tip at 8:00 p.m. ET. We'll update this page when the NBA posts semifinal matchups and times.

**Who hosts the semifinals:** the higher seed. In the East, the Pistons host if they win; if the Celtics win, they play at the Hawks or the 76ers. In the West, the Mavericks host if they win; if the Spurs win, they play at the Trail Blazers or the Grizzlies.

## How the eight teams got here

Group play ended on Black Friday, Nov. 27, with seven of the eight spots decided that night:

- **East:** the Pistons won Group A and the top seed after beating the Bucks 113-98. The 76ers won Group B by beating the Pacers 111-87. The Hawks beat the Celtics 87-86 in Boston to take Group C, and the Celtics still advanced as the East wild card.
- **West:** the Mavericks were the only team to go 4-0, finishing with a comeback 113-109 win at Phoenix to clinch Group A and the top seed. The Grizzlies won Group B with a 114-109 win at Minnesota, and the Trail Blazers won Group C by beating the Warriors 116-94. The Spurs beat the Lakers 99-96 to take the West wild card.

The Pistons, 76ers and Grizzlies are in the knockout round for the first time. Teams that didn't advance get two extra regular-season games added during the knockout week, and they'll show up on our [NBA tonight page](/nba/) once they're on the schedule.

## Why you won't find the NBA Cup on your TV channels

- **Prime Video isn't a TV channel.** It's a streaming service, so cable or satellite alone won't show these games. Open the Prime Video app on your smart TV, Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, game console, phone or computer.
- **Local channels mostly don't have them.** During group play, most Cup games were ordinary local broadcasts. The knockout games are national Prime Video games instead, so your team's usual channel probably won't carry them (ESPN's Spurs at Mavericks listing is the one exception shown so far).
- **League Pass won't work.** [NBA League Pass](/go/nba-league-pass/) covers out-of-market local games, not national Prime Video games.

## How to watch the NBA Cup knockout rounds without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** every quarterfinal, both semifinals and the championship. It's included with an Amazon Prime membership; check Amazon for current plans.
