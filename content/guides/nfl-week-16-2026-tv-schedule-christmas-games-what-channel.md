**Short answer:** NFL Week 16 is Christmas week. It opens with **Texans at Eagles on Prime Video** on Christmas Eve, then has three **Christmas Day** games: **Packers at Bears** and **Bills at Broncos on Netflix**, and **Rams at Seahawks on FOX**. ## The Christmas Day games on Netflix and FOX

Christmas falls on a Friday this year, and the NFL has a three-game slate:

- **Packers at Bears**, 1:00 p.m. ET (noon CT) at Soldier Field, on **[Netflix](/go/netflix/)**.
- **Bills at Broncos**, 4:30 p.m. ET (2:30 p.m. MT) at Empower Field at Mile High, on **[Netflix](/go/netflix/)**.
- **Rams at Seahawks**, 8:15 p.m. ET (5:15 p.m. PT) at Lumen Field, on **FOX**.

The two afternoon games are streaming games. They aren't on CBS, FOX, NBC or ESPN, so a cable package or live TV service alone won't show them; you need a Netflix account. The night game is on FOX, so [an antenna](/go/antenna/) picks it up free, and [FOX One](/go/fox-one/) and live TV services that carry your local FOX station stream it too.

## The Saturday NFL Network doubleheader

Saturday, Dec 26 has two games on **NFL Network**, at 4:30 p.m. ET and 8:00 p.m. ET. The NFL hasn't named them yet. They'll be two of **Buccaneers at Falcons**, **Bengals at Colts**, **Commanders at Vikings** and **Panthers at Steelers**, and the league says the final dates and times will be announced no later than right after Week 14. The two games that aren't picked for Saturday will be placed in another window, most likely Sunday afternoon. We'll update this page once the schedule is set.

NFL Network is a cable channel. Without cable, live TV services such as [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [Sling TV](/go/sling/), [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) carry it in some packages, so check that the plan you pick includes NFL Network.

## Monday Night Football on ESPN

**Giants at Lions** is listed on **ESPN** on Monday, Dec 28 at 8:15 p.m. ET. It streams through [ESPN's app](/go/espn/) and any live TV service that carries ESPN.

## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon Sunday games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With two CBS games and two FOX games currently set for 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, FOX has Titans at Raiders at 4:05 p.m. and CBS has 49ers at Chiefs at 4:25 p.m. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, the **Christmas games (Netflix and FOX)**, **Saturday (NFL Network)**, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 16 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[Netflix](/go/netflix/):** streams the Christmas afternoon games, Packers at Bears and Bills at Broncos.
- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets Rams at Seahawks on Christmas night, your local Sunday afternoon games and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Texans at Eagles on Christmas Eve.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Jaguars at Cowboys this week.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games, including the Christmas night game.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN and NFL Network, so it covers everything this week except the Prime Video and Netflix games.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
