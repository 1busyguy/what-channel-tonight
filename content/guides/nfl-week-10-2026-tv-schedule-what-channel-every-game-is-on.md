## The 9:30 a.m. Munich game

**Patriots vs Lions** is played at FC Bayern Munich Stadium in Germany, so it kicks off at 9:30 a.m. ET (6:30 a.m. PT), hours before the regular Sunday slate. ESPN lists it nationally on **FOX**, which means it should be on your local FOX station wherever you are in the US. You can watch it free with [an antenna](/go/antenna/), or stream it through [FOX One](/go/fox-one/) or a live TV service that carries your local FOX station.

## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With three CBS games and four FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. That's why a Bills fan in Texas or a Vikings fan in Florida won't find their team on local TV. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, the Munich game, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 10 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday games, the Munich game and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Commanders at Giants this week.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games, including the Munich game.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday morning, Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
