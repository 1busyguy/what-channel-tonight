## The games that are hardest to find

- **Streaming-only on Peacock:** Northwestern at Oregon (3:30 p.m.) is a Peacock exclusive. It isn't on NBC, so a cable box or antenna won't find it.
- **ESPN+ only:** Marshall at Old Dominion, Coastal Carolina at Georgia State, Temple at East Carolina, Louisiana Tech at South Alabama, Southern Miss at UL Monroe and App State at Georgia Southern stream in the ESPN app, not on an ESPN TV channel.
- **MW+ only:** UConn at Air Force, UTEP at North Dakota State and New Mexico at San José State stream on MW+, the Mountain West's own streaming service.
- **The CW:** Boston College at Duke (noon) and Texas State at Boise State (10 p.m.) are on The CW, a broadcast network you can get free over the air with an antenna in most markets.
- **TNT and USA Network:** Kansas State at Colorado is on TNT, and Colorado State at Utah State and Oregon State at Fresno State are on USA Network. Both are cable channels, not ESPN or FOX.
- **Conference networks:** ACC Network, SEC Network and Big Ten Network (BTN) games need a cable or live TV plan that includes that channel.

## How to watch college football Week 9 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** ABC, CBS, FOX, NBC and The CW are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) picks up Florida vs Georgia, Ohio State at USC, Minnesota at Indiana and the other broadcast games free if you're in range.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets plus the ESPN networks, SEC Network, ACC Network, Big Ten Network, FS1, CBS Sports Network and USA Network.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the TV channels on this list, including the conference networks. Check each lineup for The CW, CBS Sports Network and TNT in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and TNT. Check its lineup for conference networks and local channels, and pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the ESPN app is the only way to watch the ESPN+ games above, and it also streams ESPN's TV channels on the right plan.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** required for Northwestern at Oregon and also streams NBC's Ohio State at USC.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX and FS1 games such as Wisconsin at Iowa, Arizona State at BYU and Oklahoma State at Iowa State. **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams CBS's Minnesota at Indiana in most markets.
- **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/):** streams TNT Sports events, including Kansas State at Colorado.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
