**Short answer:** a national NHL game in 2026-27 is on **ESPN** (or ABC), **TNT** and **truTV**, or **NHL Network**, and a few national games stream only on **ESPN+**, also listed on **Disney+** and **Hulu**. Every other game is a **local broadcast** on your team's regional network, local station or team app, and streams on **ESPN+** if you live outside both teams' markets. ## Which night is on which channel

The pattern for the next two weeks looks like this:

- **Tuesday:** ESPN national games, and some ESPN+ exclusives.
- **Wednesday:** TNT and truTV, also streaming on HBO Max.
- **Thursday:** streaming-only national games on ESPN+, Disney+ and Hulu.
- **Weekends and other nights:** NHL Network picks up national games, often alongside the local broadcasts.

## Why ESPN+ shows up on almost every game

ESPN+ is the NHL's US out-of-market streaming home. If you live outside both teams' broadcast territories, nearly every game that isn't on national TV streams there. Inside your team's market, those games are blacked out on ESPN+ and air on the team's **local** broadcaster instead, which might be a regional sports network, an over-the-air station or the team's own app. Each team page shows the local channel for every game, for example the [Rangers](/nhl/new-york-rangers/), [Bruins](/nhl/boston-bruins/), [Hurricanes](/nhl/carolina-hurricanes/), [Red Wings](/nhl/detroit-red-wings/), [Oilers](/nhl/edmonton-oilers/) and [Blackhawks](/nhl/chicago-blackhawks/).

The ESPN+ exclusive games in the table above are different: they're national, so they aren't on local TV at all, even in the teams' home markets.

If you have cable or satellite, [NHL Center Ice](/go/nhl-center-ice/) is the out-of-market package for those providers.

## How to watch NHL national games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options. Lineups vary by ZIP code and plan, so check yours before you pay.

- **ESPN and ABC:** on most live TV services, including [Sling TV](/go/sling/) Orange, [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/), plus [ESPN's own streaming plans](/go/espn/). ABC is also free with [an antenna](/go/antenna/).
- **ESPN+ exclusives:** stream with [ESPN's streaming plans](/go/espn/), and ESPN lists them on [Disney+](/go/disney-plus/) and Hulu too.
- **TNT and truTV:** stream on [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/), or on live TV services that carry TNT, such as [Sling TV](/go/sling/), [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/).
- **NHL Network:** carried by some live TV services, often on a sports add-on or higher plan. Check the lineup on [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) or [Sling TV](/go/sling/) for your plan.

If you want one service for national games plus your local team, [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) carries ESPN, TNT and many regional sports networks. For out-of-market fans, [ESPN's streaming plans](/go/espn/) plus [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/) cover most games.
