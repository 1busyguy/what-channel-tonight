## Why rivalry week is confusing to find on TV

- **Games on four different days:** the MAC plays Tuesday, the Big 12 has Thanksgiving night, and many of the biggest rivalries (the Egg Bowl, Florida-Florida State, Texas-Texas A&M, Nebraska-Iowa, Minnesota-Wisconsin) are on Black Friday, not Saturday.
- **Holiday kickoffs on broadcast TV:** ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX all have Friday games, so an antenna covers most of Black Friday's big matchups for free.
- **ESPN+ only:** Kent State at Eastern Michigan (Tuesday) and App State at South Alabama (Friday) stream only in the ESPN app, not on a TV channel.
- **The CW and CBS Sports Network:** Air Force at New Mexico is on The CW, a free broadcast network in most markets, while North Dakota State at San José State, Jacksonville State at Delaware and Nevada at UNLV are on CBS Sports Network, a cable channel.
- **Mountain West Network (MW+):** Sacramento State at Hawai'i and UTEP at Northern Illinois are listed on the Mountain West's own streaming network rather than a TV channel.

## How to watch rivalry week without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and The CW are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) picks up Michigan at Ohio State, Texas at Texas A&M, Florida at Florida State, the Egg Bowl, Nebraska at Iowa, Minnesota at Wisconsin and West Virginia at Utah free if you're in range.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets plus the ESPN networks and CBS Sports Network.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry the broadcast networks and ESPN channels on this list. Check each lineup for The CW and CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN and ESPN2 for TCU at Texas Tech and Miami (OH) at Western Michigan. Pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the ESPN app is the only way to watch the ESPN+ games above, and it also streams ESPN's TV channels on the right plan.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Minnesota at Wisconsin.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/):** streams CBS's Nebraska at Iowa in most markets.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX's Michigan at Ohio State and West Virginia at Utah.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
