## Monday Night Football on ESPN and ABC

**Cowboys at Seahawks** is listed on both **ESPN** and **ABC**. That matters if you don't have cable: ABC is a broadcast network, so the game should be on your local ABC station and you can watch it free with [an antenna](/go/antenna/). It also streams through [ESPN's app](/go/espn/) and any live TV service that carries ESPN or your local ABC station. Kickoff is 8:15 p.m. ET, 5:15 p.m. PT.

## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With four CBS games and three FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, FOX has two 4:05 p.m. games (Dolphins at Broncos and Eagles at Cardinals) and CBS has two 4:25 p.m. games (Panthers at Vikings and Bills at Patriots), so your market gets one of each at most. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN and ABC)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 13 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday afternoon games, Sunday Night Football and this week's Monday night game for free if you're in range.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Chiefs at Rams this week.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Texans at Steelers this week.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
