## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Midweek MAC games:** Tuesday and Wednesday night games are on ESPN2, ESPNU and CBS Sports Network, not your usual Saturday channels.
- **ESPN+ only:** thirteen Sun Belt, Conference USA and American games, such as App State at Marshall, Tulane at Rice and Troy at South Alabama, stream only in the ESPN app.
- **MW+ only:** Nevada at Northern Illinois streams on MW+, the Mountain West's own streaming service.
- **The CW:** Georgia Tech at Clemson (12:30 p.m.), Fresno State at Texas State, UNLV at New Mexico and North Dakota State at Hawai'i (11 p.m.) are on The CW, a broadcast network you can get free over the air with an antenna in most markets.
- **FS1, not FOX:** Michigan at Oregon, Wisconsin at Maryland and Wyoming at UTEP are on FS1, a cable channel, so an antenna won't pick them up.
- **TNT:** Cincinnati at Iowa State (noon) is on TNT, which also streams on HBO Max.
- **USA Network:** Oregon State at Boise State and Utah State at San Diego State are on USA Network.
- **Conference networks:** ACC Network, SEC Network and Big Ten Network games, including Alabama at Vanderbilt (12:45 p.m.) and South Carolina at Arkansas (7:45 p.m.), need a cable or live TV plan that includes that channel.

## How to watch college football Week 11 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** ABC, CBS, FOX, NBC and The CW are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) picks up USC at Indiana, Boston College at Notre Dame, Duke at Miami, Northwestern at Ohio State, Missouri at Georgia, Texas at LSU, Purdue at Iowa, Baylor at BYU, Utah at Arizona and Friday's Illinois at UCLA free if you're in range.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets plus the ESPN networks, SEC Network, ACC Network, Big Ten Network, FS1, CBS Sports Network and USA Network.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the TV channels on this list, including the conference networks. Check each lineup for The CW and CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN and ESPN2, and Sling Blue has FS1. Check its lineup for conference networks and local channels, and pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the ESPN app is the only way to watch the ESPN+ games above, and it also streams ESPN's TV channels on the right plan.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Boston College at Notre Dame and Purdue at Iowa.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams CBS's USC at Indiana in most markets. **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX and FS1 games such as Michigan at Oregon, Northwestern at Ohio State and Illinois at UCLA.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
