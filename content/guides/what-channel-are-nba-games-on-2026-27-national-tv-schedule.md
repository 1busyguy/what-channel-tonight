**Short answer:** a national NBA game in 2026-27 is on **NBC**, **Peacock**, **NBCSN**, **ESPN/ABC**, **Prime Video** or **NBA TV**. Every other game is a **local broadcast** on your team's regional network or local station, and streams on **NBA League Pass** if you live outside both teams' markets. ## Which night is on which channel

The pattern for the next two weeks looks like this:

- **Tuesday:** NBC doubleheaders, also streaming on Peacock.
- **Wednesday:** ESPN doubleheaders.
- **Friday:** Prime Video doubleheaders, including NBA Cup group play games.
- **Monday:** NBCSN, NBC's national sports cable channel, has a game on both Mondays.
- **Other nights:** NBA TV picks up national games, often alongside the local broadcasts.

## Why your team's game isn't on the national channel

Most of the 82-game schedule isn't national. Those games air on the team's **local** broadcaster, which might be a regional sports network, an over-the-air station or the team's own app, and only inside its home territory. Each team page shows the local channel for every game, for example the [Knicks](/nba/new-york-knicks/), [Lakers](/nba/los-angeles-lakers/), [76ers](/nba/philadelphia-76ers/), [Cavaliers](/nba/cleveland-cavaliers/), [Warriors](/nba/golden-state-warriors/) and [Thunder](/nba/oklahoma-city-thunder/).

If you live outside a team's market, [NBA League Pass](/go/nba-league-pass/) streams its non-national games. League Pass blacks out games local to you, so it doesn't replace your local channel.

## How to watch NBA national games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options. Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check yours before you pay.

- **NBC:** free with [an antenna](/go/antenna/) in range of your local NBC station, or stream it on [Peacock](/go/peacock/). [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) carry local NBC stations in most markets, and [Sling TV](/go/sling/) Blue has NBC in select markets.
- **NBCSN:** a cable channel carried by [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/). Check your provider's lineup.
- **ESPN and ABC:** on most live TV services, including [Sling TV](/go/sling/) Orange, [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/), plus [ESPN's own streaming plans](/go/espn/). ABC is also free with an antenna.
- **Prime Video:** included with an Amazon Prime membership through [Prime Video](/go/prime-video/).
- **NBA TV:** carried by [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Sling TV](/go/sling/) and [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/).

If you want one service for most national games plus local channels, a live TV service such as [Fubo](/go/fubo/) or [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) covers NBC, ESPN and ABC, and DIRECTV Stream also carries many regional sports networks for your local team. You'd still need Prime for the Friday games.
