**Short answer:** Christmas Day 2026 is **Friday, Dec 25**, and it's a basketball and football doubleheader all day. The NBA has **five games on ABC and ESPN** (noon to 10:30 p.m. ET), led by **Spurs at Knicks** and **76ers at Lakers**. The NFL has **Packers at Bears** and **Bills at Broncos on [Netflix](/go/netflix/)**, then **Rams at Seahawks on FOX** at night. Christmas Eve has **Texans at Eagles on [Prime Video](/go/prime-video/)** and two bowl games on ESPN, and Dec 26 brings five more bowls, an **NFL Network** doubleheader, the NHL's return and Boxing Day soccer. Our [tonight page](/) lists every game's channel each night.

## The NBA's five Christmas games on ABC and ESPN

The NBA is playing five games on Christmas for the 19th straight year, and ESPN says all five air on **both ABC and ESPN** and stream in the [ESPN app](/go/espn/):

- **Spurs at Knicks (noon ET):** a rematch of the NBA Finals, with Victor Wembanyama's Spurs visiting the defending champion Knicks at Madison Square Garden.
- **Heat at Celtics (2:30 p.m.):** Giannis Antetokounmpo and the Heat visit Jayson Tatum's Celtics.
- **76ers at Lakers (5:00 p.m.):** LeBron James returns to Los Angeles with Philadelphia to face Luka Dončić's Lakers.
- **Thunder at Timberwolves (8:00 p.m.):** Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's Thunder against Anthony Edwards' Timberwolves. ESPN's schedule lists it at Target Center in Minneapolis.
- **Nuggets at Warriors (10:30 p.m.):** Nikola Jokić and Denver visit Stephen Curry and Golden State to close the night.

Because ABC carries every game, an [antenna](/go/antenna/) is enough to watch all five free. Team local channels don't carry these games, and they aren't available live on [NBA League Pass](/go/nba-league-pass/), which is for out-of-market regular-season games. For the rest of the season's national schedule, see our [NBA national TV guide](/guides/what-channel-are-nba-games-on-2026-27-national-tv-schedule/).

## The NFL's Christmas games on Netflix and FOX

The NFL's Christmas tripleheader is part of [Week 16](/guides/nfl-week-16-2026-tv-schedule-christmas-games-what-channel/):

- **Packers at Bears, 1:00 p.m. ET**, on **[Netflix](/go/netflix/)**.
- **Bills at Broncos, 4:30 p.m. ET**, on **[Netflix](/go/netflix/)**.
- **Rams at Seahawks, 8:15 p.m. ET**, on **FOX**.

The two afternoon games are streaming games: they aren't on CBS, FOX, NBC or ESPN, so cable or a live TV streaming service alone won't show them, and you need a Netflix subscription. The night game is on FOX, which you can get free with [an antenna](/go/antenna/) or stream on [FOX One](/go/fox-one/) or a live TV service that carries your local FOX station.

## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Netflix and Prime Video aren't TV channels.** Packers at Bears and Bills at Broncos are only on [Netflix](/go/netflix/), and Texans at Eagles is on [Prime Video](/go/prime-video/) outside the two home markets. You need those subscriptions; cable alone won't show them.
- **ABC and ESPN are both showing the NBA.** Every Christmas NBA game is on both, so pick whichever you have. ABC is free over the air.
- **ESPN vs ESPN+:** the bowl games on ESPN are on the cable channel. ESPN+ is a separate streaming tier in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/) and is where out-of-market NHL games stream on Dec 26.
- **NFL Network and NBA TV are cable channels**, not broadcast networks, so an antenna won't get the Saturday NFL doubleheader or the Dec 26 NBA TV games.

## How to watch the Christmas games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/):** picks up ABC (all five NBA games, the Pinstripe, Duke's Mayo and Cactus bowls) and FOX (Rams at Seahawks) free.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ABC, FOX, ESPN and NFL Network in most markets, which covers everything here except the Netflix and Prime Video games. Check your ZIP code for local channels.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** also carry local ABC and FOX plus ESPN in most markets. Check each lineup for NFL Network and NBA TV.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, so it gets all five NBA Christmas games and the ESPN bowls. Sling Blue has NFL Network and carries FOX only in select markets, so pair it with an antenna.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/)** streams the ESPN simulcasts of the NBA games and the ESPN bowls on the right plan, and ESPN+ for out-of-market NHL games.
- **[Netflix](/go/netflix/)** for Packers at Bears and Bills at Broncos, **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/)** for Texans at Eagles, **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** for Rams at Seahawks, and **[Peacock](/go/peacock/)** for Boxing Day Premier League games.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
