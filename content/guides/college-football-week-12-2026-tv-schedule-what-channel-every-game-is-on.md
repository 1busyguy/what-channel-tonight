## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Midweek MAC games:** Tuesday and Wednesday night games are on ESPN2, ESPNU and CBS Sports Network, not your usual Saturday channels.
- **ESPN+ only:** fourteen games stream only in the ESPN app, including a Big 12 game, Colorado at Cincinnati (noon), plus Sun Belt, Conference USA and American games such as South Florida at Florida Atlantic and Georgia State at Marshall.
- **SEC Network+ only:** Ole Miss vs Wofford, Mississippi State vs Tennessee Tech and Alabama vs Chattanooga are digital-only SEC Network+ games, which stream in the ESPN app, not on the SEC Network TV channel.
- **Friday night on FOX and FS1:** Oregon at Michigan State is on FOX at 8 p.m., while Iowa State at UCF (6 p.m.) and New Mexico at Wyoming (9 p.m.) are on FS1, a cable channel that an antenna won't pick up.
- **The CW:** UTEP at Air Force (Friday), North Carolina at Virginia (12:30 p.m.), Northern Illinois at North Dakota State and Colorado State at Fresno State are on The CW, a broadcast network you can get free over the air with an antenna in most markets.
- **USA Network:** Washington State at Texas State and San Diego State at Boise State are on USA Network.
- **Conference networks:** ACC Network, SEC Network and Big Ten Network games, including Kentucky at Missouri, Iowa at Illinois and Maryland at USC, need a cable or live TV plan that includes that channel.

## How to watch college football Week 12 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** ABC, FOX, NBC and The CW are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) picks up Georgia at South Carolina, SMU at Notre Dame, Wisconsin at Purdue, Friday's Oregon at Michigan State and the CW games free if you're in range.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets plus the ESPN networks, SEC Network, ACC Network, Big Ten Network, FS1, CBS Sports Network and USA Network.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the TV channels on this list, including the conference networks. Check each lineup for The CW and CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN and ESPN2, and Sling Blue has FS1. Check its lineup for conference networks and local channels, and pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the ESPN app is the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games above, and it also streams ESPN's TV channels on the right plan.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's SMU at Notre Dame and Wisconsin at Purdue.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX and FS1 games such as Oregon at Michigan State, Rutgers at Penn State and Northwestern at Minnesota.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
