## Monday Night Football is on ESPN only this week

**Steelers at Jaguars** is listed on **ESPN** alone, with no ABC simulcast, so an antenna won't get it. To watch without cable, stream it through [ESPN's app](/go/espn/) or a live TV service that carries ESPN: [Sling Orange](/go/sling/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/), [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/) or [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/). Kickoff is 8:15 p.m. ET at EverBank Stadium in Jacksonville. As with every national game, it usually also airs on a local broadcast station in the two teams' home markets.

## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. CBS has five games at 1 p.m. and FOX has three, and your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, CBS has a single 4:05 p.m. game (Chargers at Raiders) while FOX splits three 4:25 p.m. games (Chiefs at Bengals, Rams at 49ers and Giants at Seahawks) across the country, so your FOX station shows just one of those three. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN)** are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 14 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday afternoon games and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range. It won't get this week's Monday night game, which is ESPN only.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Vikings at Patriots this week.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Bills at Packers this week.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.
