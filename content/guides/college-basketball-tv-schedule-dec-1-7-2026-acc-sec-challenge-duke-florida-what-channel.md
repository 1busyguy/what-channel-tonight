## Why you might not find your game on TV

- **Peacock-only:** Iowa at Purdue, Wisconsin at Northwestern, California at Utah, Louisville vs Michigan, Wisconsin at Marquette and Northwestern vs BYU stream on [Peacock](/go/peacock/), not on NBC or any cable channel.
- **FOX vs FS1:** UConn vs Illinois, Arizona vs St. John's and Gonzaga vs UCLA are on FOX, the free broadcast network. Rutgers at Indiana, Oregon at UCLA, Drake at Michigan State, Virginia Tech at West Virginia and Indiana at Iowa are on FS1, a separate cable channel.
- **ACC Network and SEC Network:** Five Challenge games are on these conference channels, not on ESPN or ESPN2. Most live TV streaming services carry them, but not every plan does.
- **ESPN+ and SEC Network+:** St. John's, Kansas, Arizona and Houston home games, plus Arkansas vs Oral Roberts and LSU vs Hofstra, stream only in [the ESPN app](/go/espn/), not on a TV channel.
- **TNT and truTV:** Nebraska at Creighton, Saint Mary's at Oklahoma State, SMU at DePaul and Colorado State at Colorado are on TNT, and Kansas State at Seton Hall is on truTV. Both also stream on [HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/).
- **A late tip on the East Coast:** Gonzaga vs UCLA in Anaheim starts at 8:30 p.m. Pacific, which is 11:30 p.m. Eastern.

## How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ACC Network and SEC Network, which carry the whole ACC/SEC Challenge.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** Louisville vs Michigan and the other Peacock-only games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ACC Network, SEC Network, FOX, FS1 and Big Ten Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** and **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** carry most of the channels in the table, including ESPN, ESPN2, FOX, FS1, TNT and truTV. Check each lineup for ESPNU, ACC Network, SEC Network and Big Ten Network in your ZIP code.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2, TNT and truTV, and Sling Blue has FS1. Check its lineup for ESPNU, ACC Network and SEC Network.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams FOX and FS1, **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/)** streams TNT and truTV games, and **[an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/)** picks up FOX free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
