## Why NBC, HBO Max and truTV get mixed up

Homestead weekend splits Cup coverage across a few partners, which is why people search for one channel and land on three different answers:

- **Practice and qualifying** (Friday and Saturday) are on **truTV** and **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/)**. Those sessions are not on NBC.
- **The Championship Race** (Sunday) is on **NBC** and **HBO Max**. ESPN's scoreboard and NASCAR.com both list those two outlets for the 3:00 p.m. ET green flag.
- **Support series** fill the rest of the weekend: Trucks on **FS2/FS1**, O'Reilly practice/qualifying on **ESPN Unlimited**, and the O'Reilly Championship Race on **The CW** and ESPN Unlimited.

So if someone tells you "it's on Max," they may mean practice, qualifying *or* the race. For Sunday's title race specifically, plan on **NBC** (TV) and/or **HBO Max** (stream).

There are **no local blackouts** for Cup Series races. The Championship is national, so you don't need a special out-of-market package the way you would for a regional NFL or NBA game.

## How to watch without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options. Cup races don't have local blackouts.

- **Antenna (free):** NBC is a broadcast network. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets USA Sports Pre-Race and the Championship Race for free if you're in range of your local NBC station. The CW's Saturday O'Reilly Championship Race is also over-the-air in markets that carry The CW.
- **[HBO Max](/go/hbo-max/):** streams the Cup sessions NASCAR lists on Max — practice, qualifying and the Championship Race — and is also a path for truTV-linked TNT Sports coverage.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local NBC in most markets plus FS1/FS2 for Truck coverage. Check its lineup for truTV and The CW in your ZIP code.
- **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)**, **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/):** carry local NBC in most markets and typically include FS1 and truTV. Confirm The CW and truTV for your address.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Blue carries FOX Sports channels (FS1/FS2) and NBC only in select markets. Pair it with an antenna for the NBC race if your market isn't covered, and check whether truTV is on your plan.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/):** useful for FS1/FS2 Truck sessions if you don't need the full live TV bundle.
- **Peacock:** NASCAR.com does **not** list Peacock for this Championship Race (it lists NBC and HBO Max). Use your local NBC station or HBO Max unless your Peacock plan clearly includes live local NBC for this window.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check each service before you pay. For the broader "which channel am I actually getting?" problem, see our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
