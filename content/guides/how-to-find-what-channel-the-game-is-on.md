Every night someone asks the same question: **what channel is the game on?** In 2026 the answer depends on three things: the league, whether the game was picked for national TV, and where you live.

## 1. Check whether it's a national game

National games air on the same channel everywhere: ESPN/ABC, NBC/Peacock, Prime Video, TNT/truTV, FOX/FS1, CBS. Our [tonight page](/) lists each game's national channel first.

## 2. If it isn't national, it's local

Most NBA, NHL and MLB regular-season games are **local broadcasts**. They air on a regional sports network, a local over-the-air station, or the team's own streaming app, and only inside the team's home territory. Each team page (for example the [Cavaliers](/nba/cleveland-cavaliers/) or the [Bruins](/nhl/boston-bruins/)) shows the local broadcaster listed for every game.

For the NFL, Sunday afternoon games are on your **local CBS or FOX station**. Which game you get depends on your market.

## 3. Out of market? Use the league package

| League | Out-of-market option |
|---|---|
| NFL | NFL Sunday Ticket (Sunday afternoon games) |
| NBA | NBA League Pass |
| NHL | ESPN+ (US) |
| MLB | MLB.TV |

All of these black out games that are local to you.

## 4. Watching without cable

Live TV streaming services carry most national channels and some regional networks. [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [Fubo](/go/fubo/), [Sling TV](/go/sling/), [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) and [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/) all offer local channels or sports networks, but lineups differ by ZIP code. For free local CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC games, [an antenna](/go/antenna/) is the cheapest option.

We only list legal, licensed ways to watch.
