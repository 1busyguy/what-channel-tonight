**Short answer:** NFL Week 17 is New Year's week. It opens with **Ravens at Bengals on Prime Video** on New Year's Eve. Saturday, Jan 2 has a **4:30 p.m. ET game on NBC** and an **8:00 p.m. ET game streaming only on Peacock**, both picked from four matchups the NFL hasn't placed yet. Sunday afternoon is split between **CBS** and **FOX**, **Eagles at 49ers** is on **NBC** (also on Peacock) Sunday night, and **Texans at Packers** is on **ESPN** Monday night. No team is on bye. ## Thursday Night Football on New Year's Eve

**Ravens at Bengals** kicks off at 8:15 p.m. ET on Thursday, Dec 31 at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati, and streams on **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/)**. Outside the Baltimore and Cincinnati areas, you need Prime Video to watch it; in those two home markets it also airs on a local broadcast station. See our [Thursday Night Football page](/nfl/thursday-night-football/) for how Prime Video games work.

## The Saturday games on NBC and Peacock

Saturday, Jan 2 has two games. The **4:30 p.m. ET** game is on **NBC**, a broadcast network, so [an antenna](/go/antenna/) picks it up free, and it also streams on [Peacock](/go/peacock/). The **8:00 p.m. ET** game is a **Peacock exclusive**: it isn't on NBC, cable or live TV services, so you need a Peacock subscription to watch it.

The NFL hasn't named the two Saturday games yet. They'll be two of **Commanders at Jaguars**, **Chiefs at Chargers**, **Broncos at Patriots** and **Rams at Buccaneers**, and the league says the dates and times will be announced no later than right after Week 15. The two games that aren't picked for Saturday will be placed in another window, such as Sunday afternoon. We'll update this page once the schedule is set.

## Monday Night Football on ESPN

**Texans at Packers** is on **ESPN** on Monday, Jan 4 at 8:15 p.m. ET at Lambeau Field. It streams through [ESPN's app](/go/espn/) and any live TV service that carries ESPN.

## Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon Sunday games on CBS and FOX are **regional**. With four FOX games and three CBS games currently set for 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, CBS has Raiders at Cardinals at 4:05 p.m. and FOX has Lions at Bears at 4:25 p.m. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is [NFL Sunday Ticket](/go/nfl-sunday-ticket/) on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. **Thursday (Prime Video)**, **Saturday (NBC and Peacock)**, **Sunday night (NBC)** and **Monday night (ESPN)** are national, so they're on the same channel or service everywhere in the US.

## How to watch NFL Week 17 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

- **Antenna (free):** CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. [An HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets your local Sunday afternoon games, the Saturday 4:30 p.m. NBC game and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
- **[Peacock](/go/peacock/):** the only place to watch the Saturday 8:00 p.m. game, and it also streams NBC's Saturday afternoon game and Eagles at 49ers on Sunday night.
- **[Prime Video](/go/prime-video/):** streams Thursday Night Football, Ravens at Bengals on New Year's Eve.
- **[Paramount+](/go/paramount-plus/)** streams your local CBS games, and **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/)** streams your local FOX games.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/):** carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers everything this week except the Prime Video and Peacock-exclusive games.
- **[DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/)**, **[YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/)** and **[Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/)** also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
- **[ESPN streaming](/go/espn/):** streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
- **[NFL+](/go/nfl-plus/):** live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. New to this? Read our guide on [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
