## Why the MLS playoffs are confusing to find on TV

MLS doesn't work like the NFL or NBA, where your local channel or a regional sports network shows the game. Since 2023, every MLS match has streamed on Apple, and local TV deals went away. Two things changed again in 2026:

- **No more MLS Season Pass.** Apple folded every MLS match into the regular **Apple TV** subscription. If you search the app for "Season Pass," you won't find it. The Apple TV subscription is the thing to get.
- **Only some games are on cable.** FOX Sports has a smaller package, so a handful of playoff games also air on **FS1** (and in Spanish on **FOX Deportes**). If a game isn't listed on FS1 above, it's Apple TV only, even on cable.

There are **no blackouts**. Apple TV shows every game in every market, including your home team's.

## How to watch the MLS playoffs without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options.

- **[Apple TV](/go/apple-tv/):** the only place every playoff game is. It works in the Apple TV app on iPhone, Android, smart TVs, Roku, Fire TV, game consoles and other streaming devices, on the web at tv.apple.com, and through DIRECTV and Xfinity boxes. English and Spanish commentary are available for every match.
- **Antenna (free) for MLS Cup:** FOX is a free broadcast network, so [an HDTV antenna](/go/antenna/) gets the Dec 18 final if you're in range of your local FOX station.
- **[Fubo](/go/fubo/), [YouTube TV](/go/youtube-tv/), [DIRECTV Stream](/go/directv-stream/) and [Hulu + Live TV](/go/hulu-live/):** carry FS1 and local FOX in most markets, which covers the FS1 playoff games and MLS Cup. Check each lineup for FOX Deportes if you want the Spanish broadcast.
- **[Sling TV](/go/sling/):** Sling Blue carries FS1, and local FOX only in select markets. Pair it with an antenna for MLS Cup if your market isn't covered.
- **[FOX One](/go/fox-one/):** FOX's own streaming service, with FS1 and FOX, if you only want the FOX side and not a full live TV bundle.

A live TV bundle alone won't get you the Apple TV-only games, which is most of them. For full coverage, you need Apple TV.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check each service before you pay. For more on in-market and out-of-market rules in other leagues, see [how to find what channel the game is on](/guides/how-to-find-what-channel-the-game-is-on/).
