In-market vs. out-of-market Jaguars games

If you live in the Jacksonville area: Sunday afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional: you get whichever game your local CBS or FOX station carries, so your home team's game is normally on your local station. Primetime games are national: Thursday Night Football on Prime Video, Sunday Night Football on NBC and Peacock, and Monday Night Football on ESPN/ABC. Prime Video games also air on a local broadcast station in each team's home market.

If you live elsewhere: If you live outside your team's market, Sunday afternoon CBS/FOX games that aren't shown locally are on NFL Sunday Ticket (YouTube / YouTube TV). National primetime games are available everywhere on the network listed.
