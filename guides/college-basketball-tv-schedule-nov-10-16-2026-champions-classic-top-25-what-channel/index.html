Why you might not find your game on TV

  • Streaming-only games: Peacock has six games this week, including Illinois at Texas Tech, Virginia at Maryland and both St. John's games (Syracuse at St. John's on Sunday is at Madison Square Garden). Those games are not on NBC, so you need Peacock.
  • ESPN+ and SEC Network+: Arizona, Houston, Iowa State, BYU and Texas Tech home games on ESPN+, and Kentucky, Tennessee, Arkansas and Vanderbilt home games on SEC Network+, stream only in the ESPN app. They are not on ESPN, ESPN2 or any cable channel.
  • B1G+: Illinois, Michigan and USC each have a home game on the Big Ten's own B1G+ streaming service. Cable and live TV streaming services don't carry it.
  • TNT and truTV: Ohio State at UConn is on TNT and La Salle at Villanova is on truTV. Both channels' live games also stream on HBO Max.
  • The CW: SIU Edwardsville at Duke and New Mexico State vs San Diego State are on The CW, a broadcast network you can usually get free with an antenna.
  • FS1, not FOX: Marquette at Michigan, Northern Iowa at Michigan State, Virginia Tech vs Iowa and Xavier vs Mississippi State are on FS1, a cable channel.
  • Conference networks: ACC Network, SEC Network and Big Ten Network games, such as Seton Hall at Alabama and Long Beach State at UCLA, need a cable or live TV plan with that channel.

How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • ESPN streaming: the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2 and the conference networks, including both Champions Classic games.
  • Peacock: Illinois at Texas Tech, Colgate at St. John's, Arkansas-Pine Bluff at UCLA, Virginia at Maryland, Missouri vs Marquette and Syracuse at St. John's.
  • HBO Max: streams TNT's Ohio State at UConn and truTV's La Salle at Villanova.
  • Fubo: carries the ESPN networks, SEC Network, ACC Network, Big Ten Network, FS1, CBS Sports Network and USA Network.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry most of the TV channels in the table, including ESPN, TNT, FS1 and the conference networks. Check each lineup for The CW and CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and TNT, and Sling Blue has FS1 and USA Network. Check its lineup for the conference networks.
  • FOX One streams FS1 games such as Marquette at Michigan, and an HDTV antenna picks up The CW free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is the Champions Classic on in 2026?

The State Farm Champions Classic is Tuesday, Nov 10 at the United Center in Chicago, and both games are on ESPN. No. 6 Michigan State vs No. 2 Duke tips at 7 p.m. ET, then No. 17 Kansas vs No. 23 Kentucky at 9:30 p.m. ET.

What channel is Michigan State vs Duke on?

Michigan State vs Duke is on ESPN on Tuesday, Nov 10 at 7 p.m. ET, the first game of the Champions Classic in Chicago.

What channel is Kansas vs Kentucky on?

Kansas vs Kentucky is on ESPN on Tuesday, Nov 10 at 9:30 p.m. ET, the second game of the Champions Classic.

What channel is Illinois vs Texas Tech on?

No. 3 Illinois at No. 19 Texas Tech is Tuesday, Nov 10 at 8 p.m. ET and streams on Peacock. It is not on regular TV, so you need a Peacock subscription.

What channel is Ohio State vs UConn on?

Ohio State at No. 4 UConn is Friday, Nov 13 at 7:30 p.m. ET on TNT, in Hartford. TNT games also stream on HBO Max.

What channel is Florida vs Florida State on?

No. 1 Florida at Florida State is Tuesday, Nov 10 at 6 p.m. ET on ESPN2.

Where do I watch SEC Network+ games?

SEC Network+ is not a TV channel. Its games, including Kentucky, Tennessee, Arkansas and Vanderbilt home games this week, stream only in the ESPN app with an ESPN+ or ESPN streaming subscription.

What is B1G+?

B1G+ is the Big Ten's own subscription streaming service. This week it has Sam Houston at No. 3 Illinois, William & Mary at No. 16 Michigan and Arkansas-Pine Bluff at No. 21 USC. Those games are not on TV or in live TV streaming bundles.
