The 9:30 a.m. Munich game

Patriots vs Lions is played at FC Bayern Munich Stadium in Germany, so it kicks off at 9:30 a.m. ET (6:30 a.m. PT), hours before the regular Sunday slate. ESPN lists it nationally on FOX, which means it should be on your local FOX station wherever you are in the US. You can watch it free with an antenna, or stream it through FOX One or a live TV service that carries your local FOX station.

Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. With three CBS games and four FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. That's why a Bills fan in Texas or a Vikings fan in Florida won't find their team on local TV. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. Thursday (Prime Video), the Munich game, Sunday night (NBC) and Monday night (ESPN) are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

How to watch NFL Week 10 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna gets your local Sunday games, the Munich game and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
  • Prime Video: streams Thursday Night Football, Commanders at Giants this week.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Sunday Night Football.
  • Paramount+ streams your local CBS games, and FOX One streams your local FOX games, including the Munich game.
  • Fubo: carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday morning, Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
  • DIRECTV Stream, YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
  • NFL+: live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

FAQ

What channel is Thursday Night Football on in Week 10?

Washington Commanders at New York Giants on Thursday, Nov 12 at 8:15 p.m. ET streams on Prime Video. Like every Thursday Night Football game, it also airs on a local over-the-air station in the two teams' home markets.

What channel is the NFL Munich game on?

New England Patriots vs Detroit Lions is an international game at FC Bayern Munich Stadium in Munich, Germany. It kicks off at 9:30 a.m. ET (6:30 a.m. PT) on Sunday, Nov 15 and ESPN lists it nationally on FOX.

What channel is Sunday Night Football on in Week 10?

Pittsburgh Steelers at Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday, Nov 15 at 8:20 p.m. ET airs on NBC and streams on Peacock.

What channel is Monday Night Football on in Week 10?

Los Angeles Chargers at Baltimore Ravens on Monday, Nov 16 at 8:15 p.m. ET is listed on ESPN, and streams through ESPN's app and live TV services that carry ESPN.

Why isn't my Sunday afternoon game on my local CBS or FOX station?

Sunday afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. Your local station shows the game picked for your market, usually your nearest team. To watch an out-of-market Sunday afternoon game legally, you need NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube.

Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 10?

The Chicago Bears, Denver Broncos, Philadelphia Eagles and Tampa Bay Buccaneers don't play in Week 10.
