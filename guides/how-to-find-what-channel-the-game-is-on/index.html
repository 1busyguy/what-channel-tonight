How to find what channel the game is on

Last updated: Oct 24, 2026, 6:02 AM ET. All times Eastern.

Every night someone asks the same question: what channel is the game on? In 2026 the answer depends on three things: the league, whether the game was picked for national TV, and where you live.

1. Check whether it's a national game

National games air on the same channel everywhere: ESPN/ABC, NBC/Peacock, Prime Video, TNT/truTV, FOX/FS1, CBS. Our tonight page lists each game's national channel first.

2. If it isn't national, it's local

Most NBA, NHL and MLB regular-season games are local broadcasts. They air on a regional sports network, a local over-the-air station, or the team's own streaming app, and only inside the team's home territory. Each team page (for example the Cavaliers or the Bruins) shows the local broadcaster listed for every game.

For the NFL, Sunday afternoon games are on your local CBS or FOX station. Which game you get depends on your market.

3. Out of market? Use the league package

League Out-of-market option
NFL NFL Sunday Ticket (Sunday afternoon games)
NBA NBA League Pass
NHL ESPN+ (US)
MLB MLB.TV

All of these black out games that are local to you.

4. Watching without cable

Live TV streaming services carry most national channels and some regional networks. YouTube TV, Fubo, Sling TV, DIRECTV Stream and Hulu + Live TV all offer local channels or sports networks, but lineups differ by ZIP code. For free local CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC games, an antenna is the cheapest option.

FAQ

Why can't I find the game on my usual channel?

Sports rights are split between national networks, regional sports networks, local stations and streaming services. A game can be on a different outlet each night, and local broadcasts only reach the team's home territory.

What does out-of-market mean?

You are out-of-market when you live outside a team's home TV territory. Out-of-market packages (NFL Sunday Ticket, NBA League Pass, ESPN+ for NHL, MLB.TV) cover those games but black out the local team.

Is it legal to watch games on free streaming sites?

Unlicensed streaming sites aren't legal, and they often carry malware. Every option we list is an official, licensed service.
