The Saturday doubleheader on FOX and CBS

Week 15 has two Saturday games. Seahawks at Eagles kicks off at 5:00 p.m. ET on FOX, then Bears at Bills follows at 8:20 p.m. ET on CBS. Each one is the only NFL game in its time slot, so there's no competing game on the same network. Both FOX and CBS are broadcast networks, which means an antenna picks them up free, and FOX One and Paramount+ stream your local FOX and CBS stations.

Monday Night Football on ESPN and ABC

Patriots at Chiefs is listed on both ESPN and ABC. If you don't have cable, ABC is a broadcast network, so the game should be on your local ABC station and you can watch it free with an antenna. It also streams through ESPN's app and any live TV service that carries ESPN or your local ABC station. Kickoff is 8:15 p.m. ET, 7:15 p.m. CT in Kansas City.

Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon Sunday games on CBS and FOX are regional. With four CBS games and four FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, FOX has one 4:05 p.m. game (Jets at Cardinals) and CBS has two 4:25 p.m. games (Broncos at Raiders and Cowboys at Rams), so most CBS markets get one of the two. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. Thursday (Prime Video), Sunday night (NBC) and Monday night (ESPN and ABC) are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

How to watch NFL Week 15 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna gets the Saturday games, your local Sunday afternoon games, Sunday Night Football and this week's Monday night game for free if you're in range.
  • Prime Video: streams Thursday Night Football, 49ers at Chargers this week.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Lions at Vikings this week.
  • Paramount+ streams your local CBS games, and FOX One streams your local FOX games.
  • Fubo: carries local CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Saturday, Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
  • DIRECTV Stream, YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
  • NFL+: live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. See our Thursday Night Football page for more on Prime Video games, and our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is Thursday Night Football on in Week 15?

San Francisco 49ers at Los Angeles Chargers on Thursday, Dec 17 at 8:15 p.m. ET (5:15 p.m. PT) at SoFi Stadium streams on Prime Video. Like every Thursday Night Football game, it also airs on a local over-the-air station in the two teams' home markets.

Are there Saturday NFL games in Week 15?

Yes, two. Seattle Seahawks at Philadelphia Eagles is Saturday, Dec 19 at 5:00 p.m. ET on FOX, and Chicago Bears at Buffalo Bills is Saturday, Dec 19 at 8:20 p.m. ET on CBS.

What channel is Sunday Night Football on in Week 15?

Detroit Lions at Minnesota Vikings on Sunday, Dec 20 at 8:20 p.m. ET is on NBC nationally, and NBC's Sunday night games also stream on Peacock. Late-season Sunday night games can be flexed, so confirm the matchup the week of the game.

What channel is Monday Night Football on in Week 15?

New England Patriots at Kansas City Chiefs on Monday, Dec 21 at 8:15 p.m. ET is listed on both ESPN and ABC. Because ABC is a broadcast network, you can watch it free with an antenna, or stream it through ESPN's app and live TV services that carry ESPN or ABC.

What channel is Cowboys vs Rams on?

Dallas Cowboys at Los Angeles Rams is Sunday, Dec 20 at 4:25 p.m. ET (1:25 p.m. PT) on CBS. It's one of two CBS late-afternoon games, so which CBS stations get it depends on your market.

Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 15?

No team is on bye. All 32 teams play in Week 15: one Thursday game, two Saturday games, 12 Sunday games and one Monday game.
