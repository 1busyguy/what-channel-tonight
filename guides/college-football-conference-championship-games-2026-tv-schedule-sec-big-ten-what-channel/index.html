What channel are the college football conference championship games on? (2026)

Last updated: Nov 28, 2026, 5:01 AM ET. All times Eastern.

Short answer: conference championship weekend is Friday, Dec. 4 and Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026. Saturday's big three are the ACC Championship on ABC (noon ET), the SEC Championship on ABC (4 p.m.) and the Big Ten Championship on FOX (8 p.m.). Friday night has the Big 12 Championship on ABC and the Pac-12 Championship at Boise State on CBS (both 8 p.m.). Every FBS title game already has a kickoff time and channel, even though most of the matchups won't be set until after rivalry week. The CFP selection show follows on Sunday, Dec. 6 at noon on ESPN. The games to watch

  • SEC Championship (Sat, 4 p.m. ET, ABC). The SEC's top two teams meet in Atlanta, a day before the 12-team playoff field is picked.
  • Big Ten Championship (Sat, 8 p.m. ET, FOX). The prime-time finale in Indianapolis. Michigan at Ohio State on Nov. 28 (noon, FOX) will help settle who gets there; see our rivalry week TV schedule.
  • ACC Championship (Sat, noon ET, ABC). Opens Saturday in Charlotte, at the same time as the MAC title game on ESPN.
  • Big 12 Championship (Fri, 8 p.m. ET, ABC). The Big 12 plays its title game on Friday night at AT&T Stadium, head to head with the Pac-12 game on CBS.
  • Pac-12 Championship (Fri, 8 p.m. ET, CBS). Boise State hosts the rebuilt Pac-12's title game on its blue turf.

Why championship weekend is confusing to find on TV

  • Two nights, not one: five of the ten FBS title games are on Friday, Dec. 4, including the Big 12 and Pac-12. Most people expect them all on Saturday.
  • Overlapping kickoffs: the Big 12 (ABC) and Pac-12 (CBS) both start at 8 p.m. Friday, the ACC (ABC) and MAC (ESPN) both start at noon Saturday, and the American (ABC) and Big Ten (FOX) both start at 8 p.m. Saturday.
  • Host sites aren't fixed for every league: the SEC, Big Ten, ACC, Big 12 and MAC play at neutral stadiums picked in advance. The American, Mountain West, Sun Belt and Conference USA title games are played at the higher seed's stadium, so the location isn't known until the matchup is.
  • One cable-only game: the Conference USA Championship is on CBS Sports Network, a cable channel that an antenna won't get. The Sun Belt and MAC games are on ESPN. The other seven are on free broadcast networks.

How to watch the conference championships without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): ABC, CBS and FOX are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna picks up the SEC, Big Ten, ACC, Big 12, Pac-12, Mountain West and American title games free if you're in range of your local stations.
  • Fubo: carries ABC, CBS and FOX in most markets, plus ESPN and CBS Sports Network, so it covers all ten games and the selection show.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry ABC, CBS, FOX and ESPN. Check the lineup for CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code if you want the Conference USA game.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for the Sun Belt and MAC games and the selection show. Pair it with an antenna for the ABC, CBS and FOX games.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's channels in the ESPN app on the right plan, including the Sun Belt and MAC games and the selection show.
  • Paramount+: streams CBS's Pac-12 Championship in most markets.
  • FOX One: streams FOX's Big Ten and Mountain West title games.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is the SEC Championship Game on in 2026?

The 2026 SEC Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5 at 4 p.m. ET on ABC from Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta. ABC is a free broadcast network, so an antenna gets it.

What channel is the Big Ten Championship Game on in 2026?

The 2026 Big Ten Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5 at 8 p.m. ET on FOX from Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis. FOX One and live TV services that carry your local FOX station stream it.

What channel is the ACC Championship Game on?

The ACC Championship Game is Saturday, Dec. 5 at noon ET on ABC from Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte.

What channel is the Big 12 Championship Game on?

The Big 12 Championship Game is Friday, Dec. 4 at 8 p.m. ET on ABC from AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas.

What channel is the Pac-12 Championship Game on?

The Pac-12 Championship Game is Friday, Dec. 4 at 8 p.m. ET on CBS, with Boise State hosting at Albertsons Stadium.

Who is playing in the conference championship games?

Most matchups are decided by the final regular-season games on Nov. 27-28 (rivalry week). As of Nov. 21, ESPN lists every title game except the Pac-12 (Boise State is the host) with both teams TBD. The kickoff times and channels are already set.

When is the College Football Playoff selection show?

The 12-team College Football Playoff field is revealed on Sunday, Dec. 6 from noon to 3 p.m. ET on ESPN, the day after the conference title games.
