Why you might not find your game on TV

  • Streaming-only games: Arkansas vs Indiana at Madison Square Garden and Texas A&M at TCU are on Peacock only, not NBC, so you need Peacock. UCLA and USC's Sunday home games are on B1G+, the Big Ten's own subscription service.
  • ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra: Texas Tech, BYU and Kansas home games on ESPN+, Alabama, Vanderbilt, Texas, Tennessee and Arkansas home games on SEC Network+, and Miami's Tuesday game on ACC Network Extra stream only in the ESPN app. They are not on ESPN, ESPN2 or any cable channel.
  • FS1 vs FOX: UConn at Arizona and Butler vs Nebraska are on FS1, a cable channel. Michigan at Villanova on Saturday is on the FOX broadcast network.
  • TNT and truTV: Iowa at Xavier is on TNT, and St. John's, Creighton and Butler home games are on truTV. Live games on both channels also stream on HBO Max.
  • CBS vs CBS Sports Network: Kentucky vs Indiana is on CBS, the free broadcast network. Gonzaga's game and the Baha Mar and Greenbrier tournament games are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
  • Conference networks: Big Ten Network, ACC Network and SEC Network games need a cable or live TV plan with that channel.

How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • ESPN streaming: the only way to watch the ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU and ACC Network, including Illinois at Duke and the Players Era.
  • Peacock: Arkansas vs Indiana and Texas A&M at TCU.
  • HBO Max: streams TNT's Iowa at Xavier and truTV's games.
  • Fubo: carries ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, FS1, FOX, CBS, Big Ten Network, ACC Network, SEC Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry most of the TV channels in the table, including CBS, FOX, ESPN, TNT, FS1 and the conference networks. Check each lineup for CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and TNT, and Sling Blue has FS1 and local FOX in some markets. Check its lineup for the conference networks.
  • Paramount+ streams CBS's Kentucky vs Indiana, FOX One streams FOX and FS1, and an HDTV antenna picks up CBS and FOX free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.
