What channel is the NBA Cup on? 2026 Emirates NBA Cup TV schedule

Last updated: Nov 14, 2026, 5:01 AM ET. All times Eastern.

Short answer: the NBA Cup doesn't have a single channel. On Friday Cup Nights, two games a night are national on Prime Video and the rest are on each team's local TV channel or team stream (out-of-market fans can use NBA League Pass). The extra Cup Nights are the exceptions: Tuesday, Nov 24 has Celtics at Wizards on NBC and Peacock and Mavericks at Nuggets on Peacock, and Wednesday, Nov 25 has three games on ESPN. The knockout rounds start Dec. 4, and the Dec. 11 final is at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. Our NBA tonight page shows the channel for every game each night.

2026 NBA Cup groups

All 30 teams were drawn into six five-team groups within their conference. Each team plays everyone in its group once, two at home and two on the road.

The six group winners and one wild card per conference (the best second-place team) move on to an eight-team knockout bracket.

Why your Cup game might not be on national TV

Most NBA Cup games are regular local broadcasts with a Cup logo on them. If you live in one of the teams' home markets, the game is on that team's local channel. Some teams now air games on free local stations (like KJZZ for the Jazz, KSBI for the Thunder, KTLA for the Clippers or KUNP for the Trail Blazers), and several sell their own direct streams (Jazz+, Thunder App, ClipperVision, BlazerVision, MavsTV). Others stream locally on DAZN or air on a regional sports network you can get through cable, satellite, DIRECTV Stream or Fubo depending on your ZIP code.

If you live outside both teams' markets, the local-only games are on NBA League Pass. League Pass blacks out games in the teams' own markets, so it doesn't replace your local channel.

How to watch the NBA Cup without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Prime Video: two national games on most Friday Cup Nights, the quarterfinals and the championship.
  • Peacock: Celtics at Wizards and Mavericks at Nuggets on Tuesday, Nov 24.
  • An HDTV antenna: NBC's Celtics at Wizards game, plus games on free local stations in teams that use them.
  • ESPN streaming, Sling TV, YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Fubo or DIRECTV Stream: the three ESPN games on Wednesday, Nov 25.
  • DIRECTV Stream and Fubo: carry many regional sports networks for in-market local games (check your ZIP code).
  • NBA League Pass: every local-only game when you're out of market.

FAQ

What channel is the NBA Cup on?

There isn't one NBA Cup channel. Most Friday group play nights have two national games on Prime Video, and the rest air on each team's local TV channel or team stream (and on NBA League Pass outside the teams' home markets). The Nov 24 Cup Night has Celtics at Wizards on NBC and Peacock and Mavericks at Nuggets on Peacock, and the Nov 25 Cup Night has three ESPN games.

When are the 2026 NBA Cup group play games?

Group play nights are Oct. 30 and Nov. 6, 13, 20, 24, 25 and 27. Every team plays four group games, one against each team in its five-team group.

When and where is the 2026 NBA Cup final?

The championship is Friday, Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis, Butler's arena. It's the first NBA Cup final away from Las Vegas. ESPN's schedule lists the championship on Prime Video.

When are the NBA Cup quarterfinals and semifinals?

Quarterfinals are Friday, Dec. 4 and Saturday, Dec. 5, and semifinals are Dec. 8 and/or Dec. 9, both at the higher seed's home arena. ESPN's schedule lists all four quarterfinals on Prime Video; semifinal TV hasn't been listed yet.

Can I watch NBA Cup games on League Pass?

Yes for games that are only on local TV, as long as you live outside both teams' home markets. Local games are blacked out on League Pass in the teams' markets, so fans there watch on the team's local channel or team stream instead. Games on Prime Video, NBC, Peacock and ESPN are national broadcasts.

Who won the last NBA Cup?

The New York Knicks won the 2025 NBA Cup. Earlier champions were the Milwaukee Bucks (2024) and the Los Angeles Lakers (2023).
