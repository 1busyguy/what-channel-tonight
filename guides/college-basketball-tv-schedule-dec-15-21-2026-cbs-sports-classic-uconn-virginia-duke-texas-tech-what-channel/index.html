CBS Sports Classic 2026: times and channel

The CBS Sports Classic is a Madison Square Garden doubleheader on Saturday, Dec 19, and both games are on the CBS broadcast network and stream on Paramount+, per the event's announcement:

  • 12:00 p.m. ET: No. 12 Kansas vs Ohio State, CBS
  • 2:30 p.m. ET: North Carolina vs Kentucky, CBS

CBS stays on college basketball after the doubleheader with Gonzaga vs Michigan State at 5 p.m. ET (2 p.m. Pacific) from the Acrisure Series, per Gonzaga Athletics and the Pac-12's broadcast schedule. Because CBS is a free over-the-air network, all three games come in with an HDTV antenna if you're in range of your local CBS station.

Three games at Madison Square Garden in three days

  • Saturday, Dec 19: the CBS Sports Classic (CBS), then DePaul at No. 13 St. John's at 8:30 p.m. ET on Peacock.
  • Sunday, Dec 20: No. 10 UConn vs No. 2 Virginia at 3 p.m. ET on ESPN.
  • Monday, Dec 21: No. 16 Duke vs No. 5 Texas Tech at 7 p.m. ET, streaming only on Prime Video. Texas Tech announced in July that the game would be on Prime Video, and Prime Video's event page lists the 7 p.m. ET tip.

Why you might not find your game on TV

  • Prime Video only: Duke vs Texas Tech isn't on ESPN, CBS or any cable channel. You need Prime Video, which comes with an Amazon Prime membership or as a standalone Prime Video subscription.
  • Peacock-only: Lafayette at Penn State, Quinnipiac at UCLA, Jacksonville at USC, Xavier at Marquette, DePaul at St. John's, Portland State at Oregon and Alabama vs Iowa stream on Peacock, not on NBC or any cable channel.
  • CBS vs CBS Sports Network: the CBS Sports Classic and Gonzaga vs Michigan State are on CBS, the broadcast network. Iowa State vs Missouri State and Northern Illinois at Arizona are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
  • USA Network: BYU vs San Diego State (Saturday, 10 p.m. ET) is on USA Network, a cable channel most live TV streaming services carry.
  • The CW: Louisville's Saturday home game, SMU vs UNLV and North Carolina's Dec 21 home game are on The CW, a broadcast network you can usually get free with an antenna.
  • ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra: BYU, Vanderbilt, LSU, Texas and Miami home games against non-conference opponents stream only in the ESPN app, not on a TV channel.
  • Conference networks: Big Ten Network, SEC Network and ACC Network carry the Duke, Louisville, Tennessee, Florida and Nebraska home games. Most live TV streaming services carry them, but not every plan does.
  • truTV: UConn's Dec 15 game in Hartford and Seton Hall at Georgetown are on truTV, which also streams on HBO Max.

How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Paramount+: streams the CBS games, including the CBS Sports Classic and Gonzaga vs Michigan State, on plans that include live CBS (check the plan before you subscribe).
  • Prime Video: the only way to watch Duke vs Texas Tech.
  • Peacock: St. John's vs DePaul, the UCLA and USC home games and the other Peacock-only games.
  • ESPN streaming: the only way to watch the ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ACC Network and SEC Network, which carry UConn vs Virginia and Arizona vs Arkansas.
  • Fubo: carries CBS, ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, FS1, USA Network, Big Ten Network, ACC Network, SEC Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry most of the channels in the table, including CBS, ESPN, ESPN2, FS1, USA Network and truTV. Check each lineup for The CW, CBS Sports Network and the conference networks in your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and truTV, and Sling Blue has FS1 and USA Network. Sling doesn't carry CBS, so pair it with Paramount+ or an antenna for the CBS games.
  • FOX One streams FS1, HBO Max streams the truTV games, and an HDTV antenna picks up CBS and The CW free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.
