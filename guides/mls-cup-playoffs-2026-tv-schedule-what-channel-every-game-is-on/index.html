Why the MLS playoffs are confusing to find on TV

MLS doesn't work like the NFL or NBA, where your local channel or a regional sports network shows the game. Since 2023, every MLS match has streamed on Apple, and local TV deals went away. Two things changed again in 2026:

  • No more MLS Season Pass. Apple folded every MLS match into the regular Apple TV subscription. If you search the app for "Season Pass," you won't find it. The Apple TV subscription is the thing to get.
  • Only some games are on cable. FOX Sports has a smaller package, so a handful of playoff games also air on FS1 (and in Spanish on FOX Deportes). If a game isn't listed on FS1 above, it's Apple TV only, even on cable.

There are no blackouts. Apple TV shows every game in every market, including your home team's.

How to watch the MLS playoffs without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options.

  • Apple TV: the only place every playoff game is. It works in the Apple TV app on iPhone, Android, smart TVs, Roku, Fire TV, game consoles and other streaming devices, on the web at tv.apple.com, and through DIRECTV and Xfinity boxes. English and Spanish commentary are available for every match.
  • Antenna (free) for MLS Cup: FOX is a free broadcast network, so an HDTV antenna gets the Dec 18 final if you're in range of your local FOX station.
  • Fubo, YouTube TV, DIRECTV Stream and Hulu + Live TV: carry FS1 and local FOX in most markets, which covers the FS1 playoff games and MLS Cup. Check each lineup for FOX Deportes if you want the Spanish broadcast.
  • Sling TV: Sling Blue carries FS1, and local FOX only in select markets. Pair it with an antenna for MLS Cup if your market isn't covered.
  • FOX One: FOX's own streaming service, with FS1 and FOX, if you only want the FOX side and not a full live TV bundle.

A live TV bundle alone won't get you the Apple TV-only games, which is most of them. For full coverage, you need Apple TV.

FAQ

What channel are the MLS playoffs on?

Every 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs game streams on Apple TV, with no blackouts. Some games also air on FS1 and FOX Deportes in the US (and TSN and RDS in Canada). MLS Cup on Friday, Dec 18 is on FOX and FOX Deportes as well as Apple TV.

Do I need MLS Season Pass to watch the playoffs?

No. Apple ended the standalone MLS Season Pass after the 2025 season. In 2026, every MLS match, including the MLS Cup Playoffs, is included with a regular Apple TV subscription at no extra cost.

Are the MLS playoffs on regular TV?

Some are. The games MLS lists on FS1 include Nashville's Game 1 (Nov 22, 4 p.m. ET), Chicago Fire vs Charlotte (Nov 22, 6:30 p.m.), St. Louis vs LAFC (Nov 23, 8 p.m.), San Jose vs Houston (Nov 24, 10 p.m.), Columbus vs Inter Miami (Nov 27, 8 p.m.), the Seattle/RSL vs Vancouver Game 2 (Nov 27, 10 p.m.) and Portland vs FC Dallas (Nov 28, 10:30 p.m.). The rest are Apple TV only. MLS Cup is on FOX, a free broadcast network.

What channel is the Orlando City vs FC Cincinnati Wild Card game on?

Orlando City hosts FC Cincinnati in the Eastern Conference Wild Card on Thursday, Nov 19 at 7:30 p.m. ET at Inter.co Stadium. It's on Apple TV only.

What channel is Seattle vs Real Salt Lake on?

Seattle Sounders FC hosts Real Salt Lake in the Western Conference Wild Card on Thursday, Nov 19 at 10:00 p.m. ET at Lumen Field. It's on Apple TV only.

What channel is Inter Miami's playoff game on?

Inter Miami hosts Columbus Crew in Game 1 on Saturday, Nov 21 at 6:00 p.m. ET at Nu Stadium, on Apple TV only. Game 2 at Columbus is Friday, Nov 27 at 8:00 p.m. ET on Apple TV, FS1 and FOX Deportes. A Game 3, if needed, is Wednesday, Dec 2 in Miami on Apple TV.

When and where is MLS Cup 2026?

MLS Cup is Friday, Dec 18 at 8:00 or 9:00 p.m. ET (MLS will confirm the kickoff once the finalists are set) on FOX, FOX Deportes and Apple TV. The higher seed hosts.

What happens if a Round One game is tied?

There's no extra time in the Wild Card or Round One. A tie after 90 minutes goes straight to penalty kicks, and the first team to win two games wins the series.
