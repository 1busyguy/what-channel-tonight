Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. With four CBS games and four FOX games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. That's why a Ravens fan in Denver or a Cowboys fan in Boston won't find their team on local TV. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The night games are different. Thursday (Prime Video), Sunday night (NBC) and Monday night (ESPN) are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

How to watch NFL Week 8 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna gets your local Sunday games and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
  • Prime Video: streams Thursday Night Football, Panthers at Packers this week.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Sunday Night Football.
  • Paramount+ streams your local CBS games, and FOX One streams your local FOX games.
  • Fubo: carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday and Monday night in one service.
  • DIRECTV Stream, YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local CBS, FOX, NBC and ESPN in most markets.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
  • NFL+: live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. See our Thursday Night Football page for more on Prime Video games, and our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is Thursday Night Football on in Week 8?

Carolina Panthers at Green Bay Packers on Thursday, Oct 29 at 8:15 p.m. ET streams on Prime Video. Like every Thursday Night Football game, it also airs on a local over-the-air station in the two teams' home markets.

What channel is Sunday Night Football on in Week 8?

Philadelphia Eagles at Washington Commanders on Sunday, Nov 1 at 8:20 p.m. ET airs on NBC and streams on Peacock.

What channel is Monday Night Football on in Week 8?

Chicago Bears at Seattle Seahawks on Monday, Nov 2 at 8:15 p.m. ET airs on ESPN, which streams through ESPN's own app and live TV services that carry ESPN.

Why isn't my Sunday afternoon game on my local CBS or FOX station?

Sunday afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. Your local station shows the game picked for your market, usually your nearest team. To watch an out-of-market Sunday afternoon game legally, you need NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube.

Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 8?

The Houston Texans, New Orleans Saints, New York Giants and San Francisco 49ers don't play in Week 8.
