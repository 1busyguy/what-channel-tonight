Sunday Night Football in Mexico City

Vikings vs 49ers is played at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City, with San Francisco as the home team. Unlike the early-morning games in Europe, this one stays in the normal prime-time slot: 8:20 p.m. ET, 5:20 p.m. PT. It's on NBC nationally, so it's on your local NBC station anywhere in the US. You can watch it free with an antenna, stream it on Peacock, or use a live TV service that carries your local NBC station.

Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. With four FOX games and three CBS games at 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, FOX has a single game (Jets at Chargers) while CBS has two, so most of the country gets either Raiders at Broncos or Steelers at Eagles on CBS. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. Thursday (Prime Video), Sunday night (NBC) and Monday night (ESPN) are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

How to watch NFL Week 11 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna gets your local Sunday afternoon games and the Mexico City Sunday night game for free if you're in range.
  • Prime Video: streams Thursday Night Football, Colts at Texans this week.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Vikings vs 49ers this week.
  • Paramount+ streams your local CBS games, and FOX One streams your local FOX games.
  • Fubo: carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Sunday afternoon, Sunday night and Monday night in one service.
  • DIRECTV Stream, YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
  • NFL+: live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

FAQ

What channel is Thursday Night Football on in Week 11?

Indianapolis Colts at Houston Texans on Thursday, Nov 19 at 8:15 p.m. ET streams on Prime Video. Like every Thursday Night Football game, it also airs on a local over-the-air station in the two teams' home markets.

What channel is the NFL Mexico City game on?

Minnesota Vikings vs San Francisco 49ers is played at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City. It's the Sunday Night Football game on Sunday, Nov 22 at 8:20 p.m. ET (5:20 p.m. PT), on NBC nationally, and NBC's Sunday night games also stream on Peacock.

What channel is Monday Night Football on in Week 11?

Cincinnati Bengals at Washington Commanders on Monday, Nov 23 at 8:15 p.m. ET is listed on ESPN, and streams through ESPN's app and live TV services that carry ESPN.

What channel is Steelers vs Eagles on?

Pittsburgh Steelers at Philadelphia Eagles is Sunday, Nov 22 at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS. It's part of CBS's late-afternoon doubleheader, so which CBS stations get it depends on your market.

Why isn't my Sunday afternoon game on my local CBS or FOX station?

Sunday afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. Your local station shows the game picked for your market, usually your nearest team. To watch an out-of-market Sunday afternoon game legally, you need NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube.

Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 11?

The Atlanta Falcons, Cleveland Browns, Green Bay Packers, Los Angeles Rams, New England Patriots and Seattle Seahawks don't play in Week 11.
