What channel are the NFL Thanksgiving games on? Full Week 12 TV schedule (2026)

Short answer: NFL Week 12 is the Thanksgiving week, and it starts a day early. Packers at Rams streams on Netflix on Wednesday night (Thanksgiving Eve). On Thanksgiving Day it's Bears at Lions on CBS, Eagles at Cowboys on FOX and Chiefs at Bills on NBC. Broncos at Steelers streams on Prime Video on Black Friday, Sunday afternoon is split between CBS and FOX, Patriots at Chargers is Sunday night on NBC, and Panthers at Buccaneers is Monday night on ESPN. No team is on bye. Our NFL tonight page shows the channel of every game as the week goes on.

The Thanksgiving Eve game on Netflix

Packers at Rams on Wednesday, Nov 25 is the NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve game. It's at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, and kicks off at 8:00 p.m. ET (5:00 p.m. PT). It's not on regular TV channels nationally: it streams live on Netflix, and Netflix says the game is included in all of its plans, with a halftime show by U2. You open the Netflix app on your TV, phone or computer and pick the game, the same way you'd start a show.

The Thanksgiving Day tripleheader

All three Thanksgiving games are national broadcasts, so unlike a normal Sunday, everyone in the US gets the same game on the same network:

  • 1:00 p.m. ET, CBS: Bears at Lions. CBS also streams on Paramount+.
  • 4:30 p.m. ET, FOX: Eagles at Cowboys. FOX also streams on FOX One.
  • 8:20 p.m. ET, NBC: Chiefs at Bills. NBC's prime-time games also stream on Peacock.

CBS, FOX and NBC are all free broadcast networks, so an HDTV antenna gets all three Thanksgiving games at no cost if you're in range of your local stations.

The Black Friday game on Prime Video

Broncos at Steelers kicks off Friday, Nov 27 at 3:00 p.m. ET and streams on Prime Video. Like Thursday Night Football, Prime Video's games also air on a local over-the-air station in the two teams' home markets, so fans in the Denver and Pittsburgh areas can usually watch on a local channel too.

Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

Sunday afternoon games on CBS and FOX are regional. At 1 p.m., CBS and FOX each have three games, and your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, CBS has a single game (Titans at Jaguars at 4:05 p.m.) while FOX has a doubleheader at 4:25 p.m., so most FOX stations get either Commanders at Cardinals or Seahawks at 49ers. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube or YouTube TV.

Sunday night (NBC) and Monday night (ESPN) are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere.

How to watch NFL Week 12 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna gets all three Thanksgiving games, your local Sunday afternoon games and Sunday Night Football.
  • Netflix: streams the Thanksgiving Eve game, Packers at Rams.
  • Prime Video: streams the Black Friday game, Broncos at Steelers.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's prime-time games, Chiefs at Bills on Thanksgiving night and Patriots at Chargers on Sunday night.
  • Paramount+ streams your local CBS games, and FOX One streams your local FOX games.
  • Fubo: carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN, so it covers Thanksgiving Day, Sunday and Monday night in one service (you'd still need Netflix and Prime Video for the Wednesday and Friday games).
  • DIRECTV Stream, YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
  • NFL+: live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. See our Thursday Night Football page for more on Prime Video games, and our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel are the NFL Thanksgiving games on in 2026?

On Thursday, Nov 26: Chicago Bears at Detroit Lions at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS, Philadelphia Eagles at Dallas Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX, and Kansas City Chiefs at Buffalo Bills at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC (NBC's prime-time games also stream on Peacock). All three are national broadcasts, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

Is there an NFL game the night before Thanksgiving?

Yes. For the first time, the NFL has a Thanksgiving Eve game: Green Bay Packers at Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday, Nov 25 at 8:00 p.m. ET (5:00 p.m. PT) at SoFi Stadium. It streams live on Netflix and is included in every Netflix plan. Netflix says U2 will perform at halftime.

What channel is the Black Friday NFL game on?

Denver Broncos at Pittsburgh Steelers is Friday, Nov 27 at 3:00 p.m. ET on Prime Video.

What channel is Sunday Night Football on in Week 12?

New England Patriots at Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday, Nov 29 at 8:20 p.m. ET is on NBC, which also streams on Peacock.

What channel is Monday Night Football on in Week 12?

Carolina Panthers at Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Monday, Nov 30 at 8:15 p.m. ET is listed on ESPN.

Are any NFL teams on bye in Week 12?

No. All 32 teams play in Week 12, with 16 games from Wednesday, Nov 25 through Monday, Nov 30.
