What channel is the World Series on? (2026 Rays vs Brewers)

Short answer: FOX. Every game of the 2026 World Series, the Tampa Bay Rays vs the Milwaukee Brewers, airs nationally on FOX at 8 p.m. ET. The Spanish-language broadcast is on FOX Deportes, and you can stream every game on FOX One. The Brewers won Game 1 3-2 in Milwaukee and lead the series 1-0.

Why it's not on your Rays or Brewers channel

During the regular season most Rays and Brewers games air on local TV in each team's home territory. The World Series is different: it's a national exclusive on FOX, so local team broadcasts don't carry it, and the channel is the same everywhere in the US. You don't need to be in Tampa Bay or Milwaukee.

How to watch the World Series without cable

You need a way to get FOX. All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): FOX is a broadcast network. If you're in range of your local FOX station, an HDTV antenna gets every game for free.
  • FOX One: FOX's own streaming service, which streams every World Series game.
  • Fubo: a sports-focused live TV service that carries local FOX stations in most markets.
  • DIRECTV Stream: includes local FOX stations in most markets, along with many regional sports networks for the regular season.
  • Sling TV: Sling Blue carries FOX in select markets only. Check your ZIP code before you sign up.
  • YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local FOX stations in most markets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay.

Watching on MLB.TV

MLB.TV on its own won't stream the World Series. According to MLB, the FOX telecasts are available in the MLB.TV app only to subscribers who also sign in with a participating pay TV provider that carries FOX.

Spanish-language and radio coverage

  • TV: FOX Deportes has Spanish-language coverage of every game. Univision also aired Game 1.
  • Radio: ESPN Radio has national English-language coverage. TUDN Radio carries the Spanish-language radio call.

FAQ

What channel is the World Series on tonight?

FOX. Every game of the 2026 World Series between the Rays and Brewers airs nationally on FOX in English and FOX Deportes in Spanish, and streams on FOX One.

What time does the World Series start?

Every scheduled 2026 World Series game starts at 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT in Milwaukee, 5 p.m. PT).

Can I watch the World Series for free?

Yes, if you can pick up your local FOX station with an over-the-air antenna. FOX is a broadcast network, so the games are free over the air.

Can I watch the World Series on MLB.TV?

Only with a TV login. MLB says World Series games on FOX are available to MLB.TV subscribers who also authenticate with a participating pay TV provider that carries FOX. A standalone MLB.TV subscription is not enough.
