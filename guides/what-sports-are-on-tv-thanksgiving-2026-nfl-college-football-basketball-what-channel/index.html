What games are on TV on Thanksgiving 2026? Every channel, Nov 25-27

Short answer: Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Thursday, Nov 26, and the NFL has its usual tripleheader: Bears at Lions on CBS (1:00 p.m. ET), Eagles at Cowboys on FOX (4:30 p.m.) and Chiefs at Bills on NBC and Peacock (8:20 p.m.). College football adds TCU at No. 12 Texas Tech on ESPN (8:00 p.m.), and the best college basketball game is No. 20 Arkansas vs No. 2 Michigan State on CBS (4:30 p.m.). There are no NBA or NHL games on Thanksgiving. The holiday weekend starts Wednesday with Packers at Rams on Netflix and keeps going on Black Friday with Broncos at Steelers on Prime Video. Players Era 16 on Thanksgiving

The Players Era 16 plays eight games on Thanksgiving Day in Las Vegas: four bracket semifinals and four consolation games, split between T-Mobile Arena and Michelob ULTRA Arena. Every game is on an ESPN network. The semifinals are Tennessee vs Iowa State (3:30 p.m. ET, ESPN), TCU vs Creighton (6:00 p.m., ESPN), St. John's vs Louisville (9:00 p.m., ESPN2) and Baylor vs Gonzaga (11:30 p.m., ESPN). The consolation games are Maryland vs San Diego State (1:00 p.m., ESPN), Miami vs Michigan (3:30 p.m., ESPN2), Alabama vs Kansas State (9:00 p.m., ESPNU) and Texas Tech vs Oregon (11:30 p.m., ESPN2).

The bracket finals are Black Friday on ESPN: Tennessee vs Louisville at 9:00 p.m. ET and Creighton vs Gonzaga at 11:00 p.m. ET, and the championship is Saturday, Nov 28 at 10:30 p.m. ET on ESPN. Our Feast Week college basketball schedule has every Players Era game and channel through the final.

Why you might not find your game on TV

  • Netflix and Prime Video: Packers at Rams (Wednesday) streams only on Netflix. UConn vs Duke (Wednesday), Broncos at Steelers and the two Black Friday NBA Cup games stream on Prime Video. Neither is a TV channel, so you need those subscriptions.
  • ESPN vs ESPN+: TCU at Texas Tech is on ESPN, the cable channel. Army at UTSA is on ESPN+, which streams only in the ESPN app.
  • CBS vs CBS Sports Network: Bears at Lions and Arkansas vs Michigan State are on CBS, the free broadcast network. The Acrisure Series games with USC and LSU are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
  • FOX vs FS1: Eagles at Cowboys and Oklahoma vs Purdue are on FOX. Texas, UCLA and Pittsburgh are on FS1, a cable channel.
  • ESPN, ESPN2 or ESPNU: the eight Players Era games are spread across three ESPN channels, and three pairs tip at the same time (3:30, 9:00 and 11:30 p.m. ET), so check which channel your team is on.

How to watch the Thanksgiving games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • An HDTV antenna: picks up CBS, FOX and NBC free, which covers all three Thanksgiving NFL games and Arkansas vs Michigan State.
  • Fubo: carries CBS, FOX, NBC, ESPN, ESPN2 and FS1 in most markets, so it covers the whole Thanksgiving slate except the streaming-only games. Check your ZIP code for local channels.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream also carry local CBS, FOX and NBC plus ESPN and FS1 in most markets. Check each lineup for CBS Sports Network.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN and ESPN2 (TCU at Texas Tech and all but one Players Era game) plus TNT and truTV for the NHL. Sling Blue has FS1 and carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so pair it with an antenna.
  • Peacock streams NBC's Chiefs at Bills, Paramount+ streams CBS's games, and FOX One streams FOX and FS1.
  • Netflix for Packers at Rams, Prime Video for UConn vs Duke, Broncos at Steelers and the Black Friday NBA Cup games, and HBO Max for TNT's NHL doubleheader.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What football games are on Thanksgiving 2026?

Four, all on Thursday, Nov 26: Bears at Lions at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS, Eagles at Cowboys at 4:30 p.m. ET on FOX, TCU at No. 12 Texas Tech (college) at 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN, and Chiefs at Bills at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock. The Texas Tech game and the Chiefs-Bills game overlap.

What channel is the Thanksgiving night football game on?

The NFL's Thanksgiving night game, Kansas City Chiefs at Buffalo Bills, is at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC and also streams on Peacock. College football's TCU at Texas Tech starts at 8:00 p.m. ET on ESPN.

Are there NBA or NHL games on Thanksgiving?

No. Neither the NBA nor the NHL has a game on Thursday, Nov 26, 2026. Both leagues are back on Black Friday: the NBA has an NBA Cup doubleheader on Prime Video (Heat at Knicks 7:00 p.m. ET, Lakers at Spurs 9:30 p.m. ET) and the NHL has 15 games, including TNT's Thanksgiving Showdown doubleheader.

What college basketball games are on TV on Thanksgiving?

The biggest is No. 20 Arkansas vs No. 2 Michigan State at 4:30 p.m. ET on CBS in Detroit. No. 6 Texas vs Georgetown (2:30 p.m.) and No. 7 UCLA vs Saint Mary's (5:00 p.m.) are on FS1, Oklahoma vs Purdue is on FOX at noon, No. 18 USC and No. 25 LSU play on CBS Sports Network, and eight Players Era 16 games in Las Vegas are on the ESPN networks.

Is there an NFL game the night before Thanksgiving?

Yes. Green Bay Packers at Los Angeles Rams is Wednesday, Nov 25 at 8:00 p.m. ET and streams live on Netflix, included in every Netflix plan.

What channel is the Black Friday NFL game on?

Denver Broncos at Pittsburgh Steelers is Friday, Nov 27 at 3:00 p.m. ET on Prime Video.

Can I watch all the Thanksgiving games with an antenna?

Mostly. Bears-Lions and Arkansas-Michigan State are on CBS, Eagles-Cowboys and Oklahoma-Purdue are on FOX, and Chiefs-Bills is on NBC, all free over the air. TCU at Texas Tech (ESPN), the FS1 and CBS Sports Network games and the Players Era games need a cable or live TV streaming service.
