Why you might not find your game on TV

  • Midweek MAC games: Tuesday and Wednesday night games are on ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU and CBS Sports Network, so they won't be on your usual Saturday channels.
  • ESPN+ only: ten Sun Belt and Conference USA games, such as Old Dominion at Coastal Carolina, Marshall at Georgia Southern and Louisiana Tech at Troy, stream only in the ESPN app.
  • MW+ only: Northern Illinois at San José State streams on MW+, the Mountain West's own streaming service.
  • The CW: Duke at NC State (12:30 p.m.) and Texas State at Oregon State (10:30 p.m.) are on The CW, a broadcast network you can get free over the air with an antenna in most markets.
  • USA Network and FS1: Boise State at Colorado State and Fresno State at Utah State are on USA Network, and Hawai'i at UTEP is on FS1.
  • Conference networks: ACC Network, SEC Network and Big Ten Network games, including Oklahoma at Florida at 7:45 p.m., need a cable or live TV plan that includes that channel.

How to watch college football Week 10 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): ABC, CBS, FOX, NBC and The CW are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna picks up Miami at Notre Dame, Oregon at Ohio State, Alabama at LSU, Georgia at Ole Miss, Army vs Air Force and Friday's Nebraska at Illinois free if you're in range.
  • Fubo: carries ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets plus the ESPN networks, SEC Network, ACC Network, Big Ten Network, FS1, CBS Sports Network and USA Network.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry most of the TV channels on this list, including the conference networks. Check each lineup for The CW and CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN and ESPN2. Check its lineup for conference networks and local channels, and pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
  • ESPN streaming: the ESPN app is the only way to watch the ESPN+ games above, and it also streams ESPN's TV channels on the right plan.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Miami at Notre Dame.
  • Paramount+ streams CBS's Oregon at Ohio State and Air Force at Army in most markets. FOX One streams FOX and FS1 games such as Nebraska at Illinois and Hawai'i at UTEP.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.
