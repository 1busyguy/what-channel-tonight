Short answer: a national NBA game in 2026-27 is on NBC, Peacock, NBCSN, ESPN/ABC, Prime Video or NBA TV. Every other game is a local broadcast on your team's regional network or local station, and streams on NBA League Pass if you live outside both teams' markets. Which night is on which channel

The pattern for the next two weeks looks like this:

  • Tuesday: NBC doubleheaders, also streaming on Peacock.
  • Wednesday: ESPN doubleheaders.
  • Friday: Prime Video doubleheaders, including NBA Cup group play games.
  • Monday: NBCSN, NBC's national sports cable channel, has a game on both Mondays.
  • Other nights: NBA TV picks up national games, often alongside the local broadcasts.

Why your team's game isn't on the national channel

Most of the 82-game schedule isn't national. Those games air on the team's local broadcaster, which might be a regional sports network, an over-the-air station or the team's own app, and only inside its home territory. Each team page shows the local channel for every game, for example the Knicks, Lakers, 76ers, Cavaliers, Warriors and Thunder.

If you live outside a team's market, NBA League Pass streams its non-national games. League Pass blacks out games local to you, so it doesn't replace your local channel.

How to watch NBA national games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options. Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check yours before you pay.

  • NBC: free with an antenna in range of your local NBC station, or stream it on Peacock. YouTube TV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry local NBC stations in most markets, and Sling TV Blue has NBC in select markets.
  • NBCSN: a cable channel carried by YouTube TV, Fubo and DIRECTV Stream. Check your provider's lineup.
  • ESPN and ABC: on most live TV services, including Sling TV Orange, YouTube TV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream, plus ESPN's own streaming plans. ABC is also free with an antenna.
  • Prime Video: included with an Amazon Prime membership through Prime Video.
  • NBA TV: carried by YouTube TV, Sling TV and DIRECTV Stream.

If you want one service for most national games plus local channels, a live TV service such as Fubo or DIRECTV Stream covers NBC, ESPN and ABC, and DIRECTV Stream also carries many regional sports networks for your local team. You'd still need Prime for the Friday games.

FAQ

What channel is the NBA game on tonight?

It depends on the game. National games air on NBC, Peacock, NBCSN, ESPN/ABC, Prime Video or NBA TV. Every other game airs locally on each team's regional network, local station or team app, and on NBA League Pass outside the teams' home markets.

Is the NBA still on TNT?

No. Starting with the 2025-26 season the NBA's national TV partners are ESPN/ABC, NBC/Peacock and Amazon Prime Video. TNT no longer carries regular-season NBA games.

Are NBA games on Prime Video included with Amazon Prime?

Yes. Prime Video's NBA games, including its NBA Cup group play doubleheaders, stream for Amazon Prime members.

Can I watch NBA games for free?

Games on NBC are broadcast over the air, so you can watch them free with an antenna if you're in range of your local NBC station.

Does NBA League Pass show national games?

League Pass is for out-of-market games. National games and games local to you are not available live on League Pass, so you need the national channel or your team's local broadcaster for those.
