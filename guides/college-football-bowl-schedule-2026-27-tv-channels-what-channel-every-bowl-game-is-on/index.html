Short answer: the 2026-27 college football bowl season runs from Saturday, Dec. 12 (Celebration Bowl, noon ET, ABC) to Saturday, Jan. 2 (Liberty Bowl, 7:30 p.m., ABC), and the College Football Playoff finishes with the national championship on Monday, Jan. 25 at 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC in Las Vegas. Bowl season dates to know

  • Sat, Dec 5: conference championship games. See our conference championship TV schedule.
  • Sun, Dec 6, noon ET (ESPN): College Football Playoff selection show. The bowl matchups are announced the same day.
  • Sat, Dec 26: ABC's first bowl tripleheader (Pinstripe, Duke's Mayo and Cactus), with the Fenway and 68 Ventures bowls on ESPN in between.
  • Tue, Dec 29 - Thu, Dec 31: ESPN tripleheaders on Dec. 29 and New Year's Eve, with the Fiesta Bowl quarterfinal on TNT on Dec. 30.
  • Sat, Jan 2: ABC's closing tripleheader (Citrus, First Responder and Liberty) plus the Arizona Bowl on The CW.

Why bowl games are confusing to find on TV

  • Several networks, not one: most bowls are on ESPN or ABC, but the Sun Bowl is on CBS, three bowls are on The CW, and four playoff games (two first-round games, the Fiesta Bowl and the Orange Bowl) are on TNT and truTV.
  • TNT isn't on an antenna: TNT and truTV are cable channels. If you watch with an antenna, you'll miss those four CFP games unless you add a service that carries TNT or stream them on HBO Max.
  • Weekday afternoon kickoffs: many bowls start at 11 a.m., 1:30 p.m. or 2 p.m. ET on weekdays. That's 8 a.m. to 11 a.m. on the West Coast.
  • Teams are announced late: you can plan by channel now, but you won't know who plays where until Sunday, Dec. 6.

How to watch the bowl games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): ABC, CBS and The CW are broadcast networks in most markets. An HDTV antenna picks up the ABC bowls, the national championship, the Sun Bowl on CBS and The CW's three bowls if you're in range.
  • ESPN streaming: the ESPN app on the right plan streams ESPN's bowl games. The CW says its bowl games also stream in the ESPN app for ESPN Unlimited subscribers.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream: carry ABC, CBS, ESPN, TNT and truTV in most markets, which covers almost every bowl and playoff game. Check The CW for your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN and TNT. Pair it with an antenna for ABC, CBS and The CW.
  • Fubo: carries ABC, CBS and ESPN in most markets. Check whether your plan has TNT and truTV before you count on it for the playoff.
  • HBO Max: streams TNT Sports' College Football Playoff games, including the Fiesta Bowl and Orange Bowl.
  • Paramount+: streams the Sun Bowl on CBS in most markets.

Channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For more help, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

When does the 2026-27 bowl season start?

Bowl season starts Saturday, Dec. 12, 2026 with the Cricket Celebration Bowl at noon ET on ABC from Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta. The last non-playoff bowl is the AutoZone Liberty Bowl on Saturday, Jan. 2, 2027 at 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC.

What channel are most bowl games on?

Most are on ESPN or ABC: ESPN's 2026-27 bowl slate is 32 games across ESPN, ABC and the ESPN App. The exceptions are the Sun Bowl on CBS (Dec. 31, 2 p.m. ET) and the Poinsettia Bowl (Dec. 23), Holiday Bowl (Dec. 28) and Arizona Bowl (Jan. 2) on The CW.

What channel is the Holiday Bowl on?

The 2026 Holiday Bowl is Monday, Dec. 28 at 5 p.m. ET (2 p.m. PT) on The CW from Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego. The CW is a free broadcast network in most markets, so an antenna can get it.

What channel is the Sun Bowl on?

The Tony the Tiger Sun Bowl is Thursday, Dec. 31 at 2 p.m. ET on CBS from Sun Bowl Stadium in El Paso, Texas. Paramount+ streams CBS in most markets.

What channel are the College Football Playoff first-round games on?

The four first-round games are on campus: Friday, Dec. 18 at 8 p.m. ET on ESPN, and Saturday, Dec. 19 at noon on ABC and ESPN, 3:30 p.m. on TNT and truTV, and 7:30 p.m. on TNT and truTV. HBO Max also streams the TNT games.

What channel are the Rose Bowl, Cotton Bowl and Peach Bowl on?

All three are College Football Playoff quarterfinals on Friday, Jan. 1, 2027. As of Nov. 22 the kickoff times and channels for those three games hadn't been announced. ESPN says it will carry two of the quarterfinals on New Year's Day. The fourth quarterfinal, the Fiesta Bowl, is Wednesday, Dec. 30 at 7:30 p.m. ET on TNT and truTV.

What channel is the College Football Playoff national championship on?

The CFP National Championship is Monday, Jan. 25, 2027 at 7:30 p.m. ET on ABC from Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. The semifinals are the Orange Bowl on Thursday, Jan. 14 (7:30 p.m., TNT and truTV) and the Sugar Bowl on Friday, Jan. 15 (7:30 p.m., ABC and ESPN).

Who is playing in the bowl games?

Matchups aren't set yet. The 12-team playoff field is revealed on Sunday, Dec. 6 at noon ET on ESPN, and the other bowl pairings are announced that day, after the Dec. 4-5 conference championship games. The dates, times and channels below are already set.
