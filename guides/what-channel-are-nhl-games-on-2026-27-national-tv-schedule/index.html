Short answer: a national NHL game in 2026-27 is on ESPN (or ABC), TNT and truTV, or NHL Network, and a few national games stream only on ESPN+, also listed on Disney+ and Hulu. Every other game is a local broadcast on your team's regional network, local station or team app, and streams on ESPN+ if you live outside both teams' markets. Which night is on which channel

The pattern for the next two weeks looks like this:

  • Tuesday: ESPN national games, and some ESPN+ exclusives.
  • Wednesday: TNT and truTV, also streaming on HBO Max.
  • Thursday: streaming-only national games on ESPN+, Disney+ and Hulu.
  • Weekends and other nights: NHL Network picks up national games, often alongside the local broadcasts.

Why ESPN+ shows up on almost every game

ESPN+ is the NHL's US out-of-market streaming home. If you live outside both teams' broadcast territories, nearly every game that isn't on national TV streams there. Inside your team's market, those games are blacked out on ESPN+ and air on the team's local broadcaster instead, which might be a regional sports network, an over-the-air station or the team's own app. Each team page shows the local channel for every game, for example the Rangers, Bruins, Hurricanes, Red Wings, Oilers and Blackhawks.

The ESPN+ exclusive games in the table above are different: they're national, so they aren't on local TV at all, even in the teams' home markets.

If you have cable or satellite, NHL Center Ice is the out-of-market package for those providers.

How to watch NHL national games without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options. Lineups vary by ZIP code and plan, so check yours before you pay.

  • ESPN and ABC: on most live TV services, including Sling TV Orange, YouTube TV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream, plus ESPN's own streaming plans. ABC is also free with an antenna.
  • ESPN+ exclusives: stream with ESPN's streaming plans, and ESPN lists them on Disney+ and Hulu too.
  • TNT and truTV: stream on HBO Max, or on live TV services that carry TNT, such as Sling TV, YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream.
  • NHL Network: carried by some live TV services, often on a sports add-on or higher plan. Check the lineup on Fubo, DIRECTV Stream or Sling TV for your plan.

If you want one service for national games plus your local team, DIRECTV Stream carries ESPN, TNT and many regional sports networks. For out-of-market fans, ESPN's streaming plans plus HBO Max cover most games.

FAQ

What channel is the NHL game on tonight?

National games air on ESPN or ABC, TNT and truTV, or NHL Network, and some stream only on ESPN+. Every other game airs locally on each team's regional network, local station or team app inside its home market, and streams on ESPN+ for out-of-market fans.

Why does almost every NHL game say ESPN+?

In the US, ESPN+ streams out-of-market NHL games. If you live outside both teams' home markets you can watch on ESPN+, but games local to you are blacked out there, so you need your team's local broadcaster instead.

Where do TNT NHL games stream?

TNT and truTV NHL games also stream on HBO Max, and on live TV services that carry TNT such as Sling TV, YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream.

Can I watch NHL games on Disney+ or Hulu?

Some national ESPN+ exclusive games are also listed on Disney+ and Hulu. Those games aren't on regular TV, so you need ESPN's streaming service or one of those apps to watch.

Is NHL Network on streaming services?

NHL Network is carried by some live TV services, often on a sports add-on or a higher plan, so check your provider's channel lineup before a game.
