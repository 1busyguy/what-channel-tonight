Why Premier League games are confusing to find on US TV

NBC Sports owns all the US rights, and there are no local channels, regional sports networks or blackouts. Each matchweek is split across:

  • USA Network, a cable channel that gets most of the big games. An antenna won't pick it up.
  • NBCSN, NBC Sports' national cable sports channel. NBC Sports lists four games on NBCSN this week and says each also streams live on Peacock.
  • Peacock, which streams every game that isn't on NBC or USA Network. The Peacock-only games aren't on any TV channel.
  • NBC, the free broadcast network, which takes an occasional marquee game. It has none in Matchweeks 12 or 13.

How to watch the Premier League without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Peacock: the only way to watch the seven Peacock-only games this week, and it also streams the NBCSN games. It doesn't carry USA Network games.
  • YouTube TV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream: carry USA Network, which covers Arsenal vs City, the Merseyside derby and every other USA Network game.
  • Sling TV: Sling Blue has USA Network.

To see every game this week you need Peacock plus a service with USA Network. Channel lineups vary by provider and ZIP code (NBCSN especially), so check before you pay. For how channels work in other leagues, read how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is Arsenal vs Manchester City on?

Arsenal hosts Manchester City at the Emirates Stadium on Sunday, Nov 29, 2026 at 11:30 a.m. ET (8:30 a.m. PT). It's on USA Network in the US, which streams on YouTube TV, Fubo, Sling Blue, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream.

What channel is Everton vs Liverpool on?

The Merseyside derby, Everton vs Liverpool at Hill Dickinson Stadium, is Sunday, Nov 29 at 7:00 a.m. ET (4:00 a.m. PT) on USA Network.

What channel is Manchester United vs Brentford on?

Manchester United hosts Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday, Nov 28 at 10:00 a.m. ET. NBC Sports lists it on NBCSN, and it also streams live on Peacock.

Which Premier League games are only on Peacock this week?

In Matchweek 12, Crystal Palace vs Hull City and Fulham vs AFC Bournemouth (both Sunday, Nov 29 at 9:05 a.m. ET) stream only on Peacock. In midweek Matchweek 13, Hull City vs Nottingham Forest, Manchester City vs Leeds United, AFC Bournemouth vs Brighton, Aston Villa vs Everton and Newcastle vs Manchester United (all Wednesday, Dec 2) are Peacock-only.

Is there a Premier League game on Black Friday?

Yes. Nottingham Forest hosts Chelsea at the City Ground on Friday, Nov 27, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. ET on USA Network.

What is NBCSN and how do I watch it?

NBCSN is NBC Sports' national cable sports channel. NBC Sports lists Man United vs Brentford, Sunderland vs Tottenham, Chelsea vs Crystal Palace and Brentford vs Arsenal on NBCSN this week, and says each of those games also streams live on Peacock. On cable or a live TV streaming service, check whether your lineup includes NBCSN.

When are the midweek Premier League games?

Matchweek 13 runs Tuesday, Dec 1 to Thursday, Dec 3, 2026. Coventry vs Ipswich is Tuesday at 3:00 p.m. ET on USA Network, eight games are Wednesday at 2:30 or 3:15 p.m. ET, and Tottenham vs Fulham is Thursday at 3:00 p.m. ET on USA Network.
