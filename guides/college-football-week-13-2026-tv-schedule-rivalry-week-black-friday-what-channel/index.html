Why rivalry week is confusing to find on TV

  • Games on four different days: the MAC plays Tuesday, the Big 12 has Thanksgiving night, and many of the biggest rivalries (the Egg Bowl, Florida-Florida State, Texas-Texas A&M, Nebraska-Iowa, Minnesota-Wisconsin) are on Black Friday, not Saturday.
  • Holiday kickoffs on broadcast TV: ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX all have Friday games, so an antenna covers most of Black Friday's big matchups for free.
  • ESPN+ only: 17 games stream only in the ESPN app, not on a TV channel, including Kent State at Eastern Michigan (Tuesday), App State at South Alabama (Friday), Kansas State at Iowa State, Army at Rice, Navy at Charlotte and Tulane at South Florida.
  • The CW, TNT and CBS Sports Network: Air Force at New Mexico and Virginia at Virginia Tech are on The CW, a free broadcast network in most markets. UCF at Colorado is on TNT. Buffalo at Akron, North Dakota State at San José State, Central Michigan at Ball State, Jacksonville State at Delaware and Nevada at UNLV are on CBS Sports Network, a cable channel.
  • Conference networks: NC State at North Carolina and Pittsburgh at California are on ACC Network, Oklahoma at Missouri is on SEC Network, and Illinois at Northwestern and Michigan State at Rutgers are on Big Ten Network.
  • Mountain West Network (MW+): Sacramento State at Hawai'i and UTEP at Northern Illinois are listed on the Mountain West's own streaming network rather than a TV channel.

How to watch rivalry week without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Antenna (free): ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and The CW are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna picks up Michigan at Ohio State, the Iron Bowl, Notre Dame at Syracuse, USC at UCLA, Texas at Texas A&M, Florida at Florida State, the Egg Bowl, Nebraska at Iowa, Minnesota at Wisconsin and West Virginia at Utah free if you're in range.
  • Fubo: carries ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC in most markets plus the ESPN networks, FS1, CBS Sports Network and the SEC, ACC and Big Ten networks.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry the broadcast networks and ESPN channels on this list. Check each lineup for The CW and CBS Sports Network in your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN and ESPN2 for TCU at Texas Tech, Miami (OH) at Western Michigan and Kansas at Oklahoma State, and TNT for UCF at Colorado. Pair it with an antenna for the broadcast games.
  • ESPN streaming: the ESPN app is the only way to watch the ESPN+ games above, and it also streams ESPN's TV channels on the right plan.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Minnesota at Wisconsin.
  • Paramount+: streams CBS's Nebraska at Iowa and USC at UCLA in most markets.
  • FOX One streams FOX's Michigan at Ohio State, Cincinnati at BYU, Arizona State at Arizona and West Virginia at Utah, plus FS1's UConn at Wyoming and Penn State at Maryland.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.
