Why you might not find your game on TV

  • Prime Video: UConn vs Duke streams on Prime Video, not on a TV channel. You need an Amazon Prime or Prime Video membership.
  • Peacock-only: Vanderbilt at Ohio State is on Peacock, not NBC.
  • ESPN+ and SEC Network+: Florida's and Vanderbilt's home games are on SEC Network+, and Houston's Sunday game is on ESPN+. Both stream only in the ESPN app, not on ESPN or ESPN2.
  • CBS vs CBS Sports Network: Michigan State vs Arkansas on Thanksgiving is on CBS, the free broadcast network. The Acrisure Series and Houston's Friday game are on CBS Sports Network, a separate cable channel.
  • FS1 and FS2: Texas, UCLA, Purdue, Illinois and UConn's Nov 30 game are on FS1, and Oklahoma vs Pittsburgh is on FS2. Both are cable channels, not the FOX broadcast network.
  • truTV: Villanova's Tuesday home game is on truTV, which also streams on HBO Max.

How to watch college basketball without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Prime Video: the only way to watch UConn vs Duke.
  • ESPN streaming: the only way to watch the ESPN+ and SEC Network+ games, and on the right plan it also streams ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPNU, which carry the Players Era and Battle 4 Atlantis.
  • Peacock: Vanderbilt at Ohio State.
  • Fubo: carries ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, CBS, FS1, FS2, Big Ten Network and CBS Sports Network, depending on plan and ZIP code.
  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry most of the channels in the table, including CBS, ESPN, ESPN2, FS1 and truTV. Check each lineup for CBS Sports Network and FS2 in your ZIP code.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN, ESPN2 and truTV, and Sling Blue has FS1. Check its lineup for ESPNU and CBS Sports Network.
  • Paramount+ streams CBS's Michigan State vs Arkansas, FOX One streams FS1 and FS2, and an HDTV antenna picks up CBS free if you're in range.

Lineups vary by ZIP code, so check a service's channel list for your address before you pay. For the bigger picture, read our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is Duke vs UConn on?

No. 3 UConn vs No. 5 Duke is Wednesday, Nov 25 at 7 p.m. ET at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas, and it streams on Prime Video. It is not on ESPN or a cable channel.

What channel is Michigan State vs Arkansas on Thanksgiving?

No. 20 Arkansas vs No. 2 Michigan State is Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, Nov 26 at 4:30 p.m. ET on CBS, at Little Caesars Arena in Detroit. CBS is a free broadcast network, so an antenna works.

What channel is the Players Era 16 on?

The Players Era 16 opens Tuesday, Nov 24 in Las Vegas on ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPNU, starting with No. 14 Tennessee vs Maryland on ESPN and No. 17 Miami vs TCU on ESPNU at 3 p.m. ET. Thanksgiving Day, Nov 26: Maryland vs San Diego State (1:00 p.m. ET, ESPN), Tennessee vs Iowa State (3:30 p.m., ESPN), Miami vs Michigan (3:30 p.m., ESPN2), TCU vs Creighton (6:00 p.m., ESPN), St. John's vs Louisville (9:00 p.m., ESPN2), Alabama vs Kansas State (9:00 p.m., ESPNU), Baylor vs Gonzaga (11:30 p.m., ESPN) and Texas Tech vs Oregon (11:30 p.m., ESPN2). The bracket finals are Friday on ESPN: Tennessee vs Louisville at 9:00 p.m. ET and Creighton vs Gonzaga at 11:00 p.m. ET, and the championship is Saturday, Nov 28 at 10:30 p.m. ET on ESPN.

What channel is the Battle 4 Atlantis on in 2026?

Battle 4 Atlantis games in Nassau are on ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPNU. No. 4 Virginia vs Marquette is Wednesday, Nov 25 at noon ET on ESPN, and Virginia vs Xavier is Friday, Nov 27 at 11 a.m. ET on ESPN2. Friday's Marquette vs Texas A&M (5 p.m. ET) and Penn State vs Mississippi State (7:30 p.m. ET) games are on SEC Network.

What channel is Villanova vs Kansas on?

Villanova at No. 11 Kansas is Friday, Nov 27 at 5 p.m. ET on ESPN2, at Allen Fieldhouse.

What channel is Vanderbilt vs Ohio State on?

No. 15 Vanderbilt at Ohio State is Tuesday, Nov 24 at 7 p.m. ET and streams only on Peacock, not on NBC.

What channel is the Maui Invitational on Nov 24-25?

Tuesday, Nov 24: Ole Miss vs BYU (2:30 p.m. ET) and Providence vs Arizona (5:00 p.m.) are on ESPN2, and Clemson vs Washington (8:00 p.m.) and VCU vs Colorado State (10:30 p.m.) are on ESPNU. Wednesday, Nov 25: the championship is at 2:30 p.m. ET on ESPN, and the third-, fifth- and seventh-place games are on ESPN2.
