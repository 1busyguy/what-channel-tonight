Why NBC, HBO Max and truTV get mixed up

Homestead weekend splits Cup coverage across a few partners, which is why people search for one channel and land on three different answers:

  • Practice and qualifying (Friday and Saturday) are on truTV and HBO Max. Those sessions are not on NBC.
  • The Championship Race (Sunday) is on NBC and HBO Max. ESPN's scoreboard and NASCAR.com both list those two outlets for the 3:00 p.m. ET green flag.
  • Support series fill the rest of the weekend: Trucks on FS2/FS1, O'Reilly practice/qualifying on ESPN Unlimited, and the O'Reilly Championship Race on The CW and ESPN Unlimited.

So if someone tells you "it's on Max," they may mean practice, qualifying or the race. For Sunday's title race specifically, plan on NBC (TV) and/or HBO Max (stream).

There are no local blackouts for Cup Series races. The Championship is national, so you don't need a special out-of-market package the way you would for a regional NFL or NBA game.

How to watch without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options. Cup races don't have local blackouts.

  • Antenna (free): NBC is a broadcast network. An HDTV antenna gets USA Sports Pre-Race and the Championship Race for free if you're in range of your local NBC station. The CW's Saturday O'Reilly Championship Race is also over-the-air in markets that carry The CW.
  • HBO Max: streams the Cup sessions NASCAR lists on Max — practice, qualifying and the Championship Race — and is also a path for truTV-linked TNT Sports coverage.
  • Fubo: carries local NBC in most markets plus FS1/FS2 for Truck coverage. Check its lineup for truTV and The CW in your ZIP code.
  • YouTube TV, DIRECTV Stream and Hulu + Live TV: carry local NBC in most markets and typically include FS1 and truTV. Confirm The CW and truTV for your address.
  • Sling TV: Sling Blue carries FOX Sports channels (FS1/FS2) and NBC only in select markets. Pair it with an antenna for the NBC race if your market isn't covered, and check whether truTV is on your plan.
  • FOX One: useful for FS1/FS2 Truck sessions if you don't need the full live TV bundle.
  • Peacock: NASCAR.com does not list Peacock for this Championship Race (it lists NBC and HBO Max). Use your local NBC station or HBO Max unless your Peacock plan clearly includes live local NBC for this window.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check each service before you pay. For the broader "which channel am I actually getting?" problem, see our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel is the 2026 NASCAR Cup Series Championship on?

The Cup Series Championship Race at Homestead-Miami Speedway is Sunday, Nov 8 at 3:00 p.m. ET on NBC and HBO Max. USA Sports Pre-Race starts at 2:00 p.m. ET on NBC.

Is the NASCAR Championship on HBO Max or NBC?

Both. NASCAR.com and ESPN list the Championship Race on NBC and HBO Max. Practice and qualifying earlier in the weekend are on truTV and HBO Max, not NBC. The green-flag race itself is the NBC broadcast plus the HBO Max stream.

When is Cup practice and qualifying at Homestead?

Cup practice is Friday, Nov 6 at 5:30 p.m. ET on truTV and HBO Max. Cup qualifying is Saturday, Nov 7 at 2:30 p.m. ET on truTV and HBO Max.

What channel are the Truck and O'Reilly championship races on?

The Craftsman Truck Series Championship Race is Friday, Nov 6 at 7:30 p.m. ET on FS1. The O'Reilly Auto Parts Series Championship Race is Saturday, Nov 7 at 5:00 p.m. ET on The CW and ESPN Unlimited.

Are there local blackouts for the NASCAR Cup Championship?

No. Cup Series races are national. There are no local blackouts, so the race is on NBC (and HBO Max) everywhere in the US.

Can I watch the NASCAR Championship free with an antenna?

Yes for the race. NBC is a free broadcast network, so an HDTV antenna picks up the USA Sports Pre-Race and the Championship Race if you're in range of your local NBC station. Practice, qualifying, FS1 Truck coverage and The CW race need cable, satellite or a streaming service.

Do I need Peacock for the NASCAR Championship?

NASCAR.com lists the Championship Race on NBC and HBO Max, not Peacock. For the NBC broadcast, use your local NBC station (antenna or a live TV service) or the HBO Max stream. Check Peacock's own listings only if your plan advertises live local NBC.
