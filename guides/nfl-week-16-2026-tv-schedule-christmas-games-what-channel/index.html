What channel is every NFL Week 16 game on? (2026 Christmas week)

Short answer: NFL Week 16 is Christmas week. It opens with Texans at Eagles on Prime Video on Christmas Eve, then has three Christmas Day games: Packers at Bears and Bills at Broncos on Netflix, and Rams at Seahawks on FOX. Saturday, Dec 26 has an NFL Network doubleheader picked from four matchups that are still to be announced. Sunday afternoon is split between CBS and FOX, Jaguars at Cowboys is on NBC (also on Peacock) Sunday night, and Giants at Lions is on ESPN Monday night. No team is on bye. The Christmas Day games on Netflix and FOX

Christmas falls on a Friday this year, and the NFL has a three-game slate:

  • Packers at Bears, 1:00 p.m. ET (noon CT) at Soldier Field, on Netflix.
  • Bills at Broncos, 4:30 p.m. ET (2:30 p.m. MT) at Empower Field at Mile High, on Netflix.
  • Rams at Seahawks, 8:15 p.m. ET (5:15 p.m. PT) at Lumen Field, on FOX.

The two afternoon games are streaming games. They aren't on CBS, FOX, NBC or ESPN, so a cable package or live TV service alone won't show them; you need a Netflix account. The night game is on FOX, so an antenna picks it up free, and FOX One and live TV services that carry your local FOX station stream it too.

The Saturday NFL Network doubleheader

Saturday, Dec 26 has two games on NFL Network, at 4:30 p.m. ET and 8:00 p.m. ET. The NFL hasn't named them yet. They'll be two of Buccaneers at Falcons, Bengals at Colts, Commanders at Vikings and Panthers at Steelers, and the league says the final dates and times will be announced no later than right after Week 14. The two games that aren't picked for Saturday will be placed in another window, most likely Sunday afternoon. We'll update this page once the schedule is set.

NFL Network is a cable channel. Without cable, live TV services such as Fubo, Sling TV, YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV and DIRECTV Stream carry it in some packages, so check that the plan you pick includes NFL Network.

Monday Night Football on ESPN

Giants at Lions is listed on ESPN on Monday, Dec 28 at 8:15 p.m. ET. It streams through ESPN's app and any live TV service that carries ESPN.

Why your Sunday game may not be on your CBS or FOX station

The 1 p.m. and late-afternoon Sunday games on CBS and FOX are regional. With two CBS games and two FOX games currently set for 1 p.m., your local station only shows the one picked for your area, usually the closest team's game. In the late window, FOX has Titans at Raiders at 4:05 p.m. and CBS has 49ers at Chiefs at 4:25 p.m. The legal way to watch out-of-market Sunday afternoon games is NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube or YouTube TV.

The other windows are different. Thursday (Prime Video), the Christmas games (Netflix and FOX), Saturday (NFL Network), Sunday night (NBC) and Monday night (ESPN) are national, so they're on the same channel everywhere in the US.

How to watch NFL Week 16 without cable

All of these are legal, licensed options:

  • Netflix: streams the Christmas afternoon games, Packers at Bears and Bills at Broncos.
  • Antenna (free): CBS, FOX and NBC are broadcast networks. An HDTV antenna gets Rams at Seahawks on Christmas night, your local Sunday afternoon games and Sunday Night Football for free if you're in range.
  • Prime Video: streams Thursday Night Football, Texans at Eagles on Christmas Eve.
  • Peacock: streams NBC's Sunday Night Football, Jaguars at Cowboys this week.
  • Paramount+ streams your local CBS games, and FOX One streams your local FOX games, including the Christmas night game.
  • Fubo: carries local CBS, FOX and NBC stations in most markets plus ESPN and NFL Network, so it covers everything this week except the Prime Video and Netflix games.
  • DIRECTV Stream, YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV also carry local broadcast stations and ESPN in most markets.
  • Sling TV: Sling Orange has ESPN for Monday Night Football. Sling Blue carries FOX and NBC only in select markets, so check your ZIP code.
  • ESPN streaming: streams ESPN's Monday Night Football.
  • NFL+: live local and primetime games on phones and tablets.

Local channel lineups depend on your ZIP code, so check the service's lineup for your address before you pay. See our Thursday Night Football page for more on Prime Video games, and our guide on how to find what channel the game is on.

FAQ

What channel are the NFL Christmas Day games on in 2026?

There are three games on Friday, Dec 25. Green Bay Packers at Chicago Bears (1:00 p.m. ET) and Buffalo Bills at Denver Broncos (4:30 p.m. ET) stream on Netflix. Los Angeles Rams at Seattle Seahawks (8:15 p.m. ET) is on FOX.

Do I need Netflix to watch the NFL Christmas games?

For the two afternoon games, Packers at Bears and Bills at Broncos, Netflix is the national home, so you need a Netflix subscription. The Christmas night game, Rams at Seahawks, is on FOX, a broadcast network you can get free with an antenna.

What channel is Thursday Night Football on Christmas Eve?

Houston Texans at Philadelphia Eagles on Thursday, Dec 24 at 8:15 p.m. ET streams on Prime Video. Like every Thursday Night Football game, it also airs on a local over-the-air station in the two teams' home markets.

What are the Saturday NFL games in Week 16?

The NFL has two Saturday, Dec 26 games on NFL Network at 4:30 p.m. ET and 8:00 p.m. ET. They will be two of these four matchups: Buccaneers at Falcons, Bengals at Colts, Commanders at Vikings and Panthers at Steelers. The league says it will announce the final dates and times no later than right after Week 14. The other two games move to a Sunday or other open window.

What channel is Sunday Night Football on in Week 16?

Jacksonville Jaguars at Dallas Cowboys on Sunday, Dec 27 at 8:20 p.m. ET is on NBC, and NBC's Sunday night games also stream on Peacock. Late-season Sunday night games can be flexed, so confirm the matchup the week of the game.

What channel is Monday Night Football on in Week 16?

New York Giants at Detroit Lions on Monday, Dec 28 at 8:15 p.m. ET is listed on ESPN. You can stream it through ESPN's app or any live TV service that carries ESPN.

Which NFL teams are on bye in Week 16?

No team is on bye. All 32 teams play in Week 16.
