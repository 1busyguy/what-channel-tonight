In-market vs. out-of-market

In your team's market: College football games aren't sold by market the way pro games are. Each game is on the national or conference network listed (ESPN family, FOX, CBS, NBC/Peacock, Big Ten Network, SEC Network, ACC Network, CW and others), and some games stream only on ESPN+ or a conference service.

Out of market: If you don't have cable, pick a live TV streaming service that carries the network listed, or ESPN's streaming plans for ESPN+ and ESPN-family games.
